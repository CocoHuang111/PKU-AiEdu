Learning goals encompass both exploring and critiquing AI technologies.

## Course Info:

- Fall 2024
- Instructor: Gerui Wang

## Pedagogy:

In these learning goals, instructor Gerui Wang lays the groundwork for a class in which students create images and short films with AI in order to understand the strengths, the shortcomings, and the racial biases of this technology. You can also explore an [example assignment](https://ctl.stanford.edu/aimes/aimes-library/art-meets-ai-short-assignment) from "Art Meets AI." This course and the online exhibit that students created were featured in [this article](https://ctl.stanford.edu/news/art-meets-ai-new-stanford-course-and-website).

## Learning Goals

- Develop a holistic approach to AI by experimenting with and critiquing on benefits and limitations of generative AI models.
- Analyze major mediums, practices, and artists in contemporary East Asian art and integrate decolonial approaches to art history and area studies.
- Apply visual analysis skills and formulate well-structured arguments about art works.
- Explore how AI alters perceptions of human bodies and experience through posthuman theories, assessing the socio-cultural and ethical dimensions of AI.
- Situate AI in the history of human entanglement with technologies, analyzing new directions of creativity and storytelling.
- Reflect on your own life experience and aesthetic preferences to create meaningful works.

## Assignment：Mid-term group project

Students learn to both create with and critique AI in this midterm group project.

## Course Info:

- ARTHIST 254A / EASTASN 254A: "Art Meets AI: Algorithmic Bodies in East Asia"
- Fall 2024
- Instructor: Gerui Wang

## Pedagogy:

In this assignment, students leverage capabilities of AI tools to create virtual exhibits that would not otherwise be possible. This course and the online exhibit that students created were featured in [this article](https://ctl.stanford.edu/news/art-meets-ai-new-stanford-course-and-website).

## Mid-term group project

Imagine you are a museum curator. Each team designs an art exhibition. Include artworks by both human artists (can be living or historical figure) and AI-generated images.

### Step 1:

Discuss and decide on a theme of your exhibition.

### Step 2:

Select around six artworks created by human artists to show in your exhibition. Create three artworks using AI image- or video-generation tools. Organize your artworks so that they can have dialogue with each other. Think about questions such as what you hope your audience feel, think, appreciate, or take away from your exhibition. What does this exhibition mean to your life experiences? How does this exhibition comment on social/cultural happenings around you?

### Step 3:

Make a 15-20 min presentation imagining you are giving your audience a tour of your show.
