## Course Description

Becoming the Next [*Bill Nye*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Nye) is about using video production techniques to develop your ability to engagingly convey your passions for science, technology, engineering, and / or math. You’ll have the opportunity to script and on-screen host 5-minute YouTube science, technology, engineering, and / or math-related shows to inspire youth to consider a future in science.
