## Readings

Readings will consist of a mixture of textbooks, papers, and course notes. If a reading is not specified here, please read the lecture notes associated with each class.

### Session 2: Setting and Equilibria: The Nash Equilibrium

Nash, John. [“Non-cooperative games.”](https://doi.org/10.1287/moor.2022.0149) *Annals of Mathematics* 54, no. 2 (1951): 286–95.

### Session 3: Setting and Equilibria: The Correlated Equilibrium

Von Stengel, Bernhard. [“Zero-sum games and linear programming duality."](https://doi.org/10.1287/moor.2022.0149) *Mathematics of Operations Research* 49, no. 2 (2024): 1091–1108.

### Session 4: Learning in Games: Foundations

Introduction to Blum, Avrim, and Yishay Mansour. [“From external to internal regret.” (PDF)](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume8/blum07a/blum07a.pdf) *Journal of Machine Learning Research* 8, no. 6 (2007).

### Session 6: Learning in Games: Algorithms (part II)

Syrgkanis, Vasilis, Alekh Agarwal, Haipeng Luo, and Robert E. Schapire. “[Fast convergence of regularized learning in games](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.00407).” *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems* 28 (2015).

### Session 7: Learning in Games: Bandit Feedback

Zimmert, Julian, and Yevgeny Seldin. [“Tsallis-inf: An optimal algorithm for stochastic and adversarial bandits.” (PDF)](https://jmlr.csail.mit.edu/papers/volume22/19-753/19-753.pdf) *Journal of Machine Learning Research* 22, no. 28 (2021): 1–49.

### Session 8: Learning in Games: Φ-Regret Minimization

Gordon, Geoffrey J., Amy Greenwald, and Casey Marks. “[No-regret learning in convex games](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1390156.1390202).” In *Proceedings of the 25th International Conference on Machine Learning*, pp. 360–367 (2008).

Blum, Avrim, and Yishay Mansour. [“From external to internal regret.” (PDF)](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume8/blum07a/blum07a.pdf) *Journal of Machine Learning Research* 8, no. 6 (2007).

### Session 18: Combinatorial Games and Kernelized MWU (Part I)

Farina, Gabriele, Chung-Wei Lee, Haipeng Luo, and Christian Kroer. [“Kernelized multiplicative weights for 0/1-polyhedral games: Bridging the gap between learning in extensive-form and normal-form games.” (PDF)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/farina22a/farina22a.pdf) In *International Conference on Machine Learning* (2022): 6337–6357.

### Session 20: Computation of Exact Equilibria

Papadimitriou, Christos H., and Tim Roughgarden. “[Computing correlated equilibria in multi-player games](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1379759.1379762).” *Journal of the ACM (JACM)* 55, no. 3 (2008): 1–29.

Jiang, Albert Xin, and Kevin Leyton-Brown. “[Polynomial-time computation of exact correlated equilibrium in compact games](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geb.2013.02.002).” *Games and Economic Behavior* 91 (2015): 347–359.

Farina, Gabriele, and Charilaos Pipis. “[Polynomial-time computation of exact Φ-equilibria in polyhedral games](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.16316).” *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems* 37 (2024): 4207–4234.
