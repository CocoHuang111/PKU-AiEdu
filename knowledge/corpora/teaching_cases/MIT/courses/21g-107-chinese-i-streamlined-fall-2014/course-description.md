## Course Description

This course, along with [21G.108 / 158](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21g-108-chinese-ii-streamlined-spring-2015/) offered in the spring, form the elementary level of the streamlined sequence, which is for students who have some basic conversational skills gained, typically, from growing up in a Chinese speaking environment, but lack a corresponding level of literacy. The focus of the course is on learning standard everyday usage, on reading in both full and simplified characters, and on writing. This course, along with [21G.108 / 158](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21g-108-chinese-ii-streamlined-spring-2015/) offered in the spring, are conducted entirely in Chinese.
