| 4 | Read the JMA paper “ <br> [The Facts on Protection Needs in Humanitarian Demining](http://maic.jmu.edu/journal/4.2/Focus/PN/protectneeds.htm) <br> ” by Andy Smith. <br> Follow this with the JMA paper “ <br> [What Use is a Database of Demining Accidents?](http://maic.jmu.edu/journal/6.2/notes/andysmith/andysmith.htm) <br> ” also by Andy Smith. <br> Read the JMA paper “ <br> [Women Deminers in Croatia](http://maic.jmu.edu/journal/7.2/focus/townsend/townsend.htm) <br> ” by Jennette Townsend. <br> Read the JMA paper “ <br> [First Person: Deminers’ Stories](http://maic.jmu.edu/journal/7.1/focus/huntington/huntington.htm) <br> ” by Huntington Associates. |
