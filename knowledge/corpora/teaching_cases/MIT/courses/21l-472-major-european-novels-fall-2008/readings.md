## Readings

### Required Books

de Cervantes, Miguel. *Don Quixote*. Excerpts from translation by John Ormsby ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21l-472-major-european-novels-fall-2008/resources/don_quixote_exce/)) ([Download](http://www.gutenberg.org/etext/996) the complete work from Project Gutenberg.)

de Balzac, Honore. *Père Goriot*. Translated by Henry Reed. New York, NY: Signet Classics, 2004. ISBN: 9780451529596. (Version used in class, translated by Ellen Marriage ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21l-472-major-european-novels-fall-2008/resources/pere_goriot/)).)

Stendhal. *The Red and the Black*. Translated by Lloyd C. Parks. New York, NY: Signet Classics, 2006. ISBN: 9780451530288.

Flaubert, Gustave. *Madame Bovary*. Translated by Eleanor Marx-Aveling. New York, NY: Barnes & Noble Classics, 2005. ISBN: 9781593080525. ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21l-472-major-european-novels-fall-2008/resources/madame_bovary/)) ([Download](http://www.gutenberg.org/etext/2413) this translation in other formats from Project Gutenberg.)

Dostoyevsky, Fyodor. *Crime and Punishment*. Translated by Richard Pevear and Larissa Volokhonsky. New York, NY: Vintage, 1993. ISBN: 9780679734505. ([Download](http://www.gutenberg.org/etext/2554) a different translation by Constance Garnett from Project Gutenberg.)

Tolstoy, Leo. *Anna Karenina*. Translated by David Magarshack. New York, NY: Signet Classics, 2002. ISBN: 9780451528612. ([Download](http://www.gutenberg.org/etext/1399) a different translation by Constance Garnett from Project Gutenberg.)
