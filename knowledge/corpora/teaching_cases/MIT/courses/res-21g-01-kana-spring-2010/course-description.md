## Course Description

Learning hiragana and katakana is an important part of reading and speaking Japanese. The following pages contain:

[Hiragana](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-21g-01-kana-spring-2010/pages/hiragana) - stroke order [videos](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-21g-01-kana-spring-2010/pages/hiragana), pronunciation, and vocabulary for each character; reading and listening [audio exercises](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-21g-01-kana-spring-2010/pages/hiragana); [handouts](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-21g-01-kana-spring-2010/pages/hiragana) on how to construct words and sentences; interactive [quizzes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-21g-01-kana-spring-2010/pages/hiragana) testing character recognition; and printable [worksheets](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-21g-01-kana-spring-2010/pages/hiragana) to practice writing characters.

[Katakana](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-21g-01-kana-spring-2010/pages/katakana) - pronunciation and vocabulary for each [character](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-21g-01-kana-spring-2010/pages/katakana); reading and listening [audio exercises](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-21g-01-kana-spring-2010/pages/katakana); interactive [quizzes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-21g-01-kana-spring-2010/pages/katakana) testing character and vocabulary recognition; and printable [worksheets](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-21g-01-kana-spring-2010/pages/katakana) to practice writing characters.

These materials were developed as part of the Japanese curriculum at MIT for students of all levels to learn and review. Students and instructors are encouraged to incorporate them directly or as supplements in their study of Japanese.
