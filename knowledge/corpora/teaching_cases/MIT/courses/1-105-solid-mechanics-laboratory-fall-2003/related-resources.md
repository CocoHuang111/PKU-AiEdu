## Related Resources

The following links highlight the concepts covered in 1.105.

- [Java® Applets](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/1-050-solid-mechanics-fall-2004/pages/tools). Interactive simulators from 1.050.
- [National Institute of Standards and Technology, (NIST).](http://www.bfrl.nist.gov/861/) Description of research areas sponsored by the Materials and Construction Research Division, Building and Fire Research Lab.
- [University of Wyoming’s Structural Research Lab](http://www.uwyo.edu/civil/research/high-bay/index.html). Several videos of lab experiments.
- [Kobe Earthquake.](http://www.iitk.ac.in/nicee/wcee/article/13_415.pdf) Structural damage to highway and bridges due to the Kobe earthquake of January 1995.
