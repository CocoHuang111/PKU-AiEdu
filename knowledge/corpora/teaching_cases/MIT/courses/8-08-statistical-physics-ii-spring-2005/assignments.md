## Assignments

Homework is handed out every week and is due one week later in class. Along with the homework is suggested reading in the required text: Huang, Kerson. *Introduction to Statistical Physics.* London, UK: Taylor & Francis, 2001. ISBN: 9780748409419. There are 12 homework assignments, plus a list of suggested practice problems for each of the quizzes and the final exam.
