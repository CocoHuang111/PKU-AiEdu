## Calendar

Each of the eight main topics for this course cuts across several lecture sessions.

| TOPIC # | Topics |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Statistical Ensembles <br> Microcanonical Ensemble <br> - Concept of Entropy and Temperature <br> - Spins in Magnetic Fields <br> Canonical Ensemble <br> - Classical Gas <br> Grand Canonical Ensemble <br> - Chemical Potential <br> - Equilibration of Chemical Reactions <br> van der Waals Model and Phase Transitions |
| 2 | Ising Model and Phase Transition <br> Meanfield Theory and Second Order Phase Transition <br> Symmetry Breaking and Phase Transition <br> Symmetry Breaking and Ginsburg-Landau Theory <br> Spins in Magnetic Field and First Order Phase Transition <br> Water-vapor-ice <br> Liquid Crystals |
| 3 | Fluctuations <br> Thermal Fluctuations <br> Fluctuation-Response Relation <br> Coherent Length and Local Fluctuations of Order Parameter |
| 4 | Quantum Ideal Gas: Bose Gas and Bose Condensation <br> Photon Gas and Black Body Radiation <br> Phonon Gas and Thermal Properties of Solids <br> Bose Condensation |
| 5 | Interacting Bose Gas <br> Wave Function of Condensed Bosons <br> Gross-Pitaevsky Equation <br> Ginsburg-Landau Theory for Superfluid <br> Collective Excitation in Superfluids <br> Superfluidity |
| 6 | Quantum Ideal Gas: Fermi Gas <br> Metal and Fermi Liquid <br> Semiconductors <br> White Dwarf and Neutron Stars |
| 7 | Transport <br> Hydrodynamics and Euler’s Equation <br> Sound Waves <br> Diffusion and Random Walks <br> Damping of Sound Waves by Diffusion <br> Viscosity and Navier-Stokes Equation <br> Heat Conduction <br> Transport in Metal and Drude Model <br> Hall Effect <br> Boltzmann Equation and Thermoelectric Effects |
| 8 | Noises <br> Fluctuation-Dissipation Relation <br> Langevin Equation-Dynamics of Random Walks <br> Power Spectrum and Noises <br> Noises in RC Circuit |
