## Calendar

The calendar for this course is less structured than those of more traditional courses. Since this course is a workshop, and involves travel, topics of discussion and research are listed around visits to Foshan.

| WEEK # | TOPICS |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Introduction |
| 2 | Initial Site Visit to Foshan |
| 3-6 | Housing and Village Preservation <br> Heritage and Cultural Infrastructure <br> Parks and Open Space <br> Transportation Systems and Networks <br> Water Resource Management |
| 7 | Mid-term Trip to Foshan |
| 8-13 | Best Practices and Case Studies in the Subject Areas (listed above) from Other Cities around China, Asia, and the Rest of the World <br> Design Charrettes with Experts from the Fields of Urban Design, Planning, and Land Policy <br> Feasibility and Implementation Considerations |
| 14 | Final Trip to Present Work |
