## Assignments Tips for Leading Discussions

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Preparation | Set clear roles (e.g. which partner will begin/end the discussion) <br> Bring your own notes and responses for helpful reference <br> Introduce yourselves at the start of the discussion <br> Clearly establish the focus of the conversation |
| Thoughtful Questions | Offer thought provoking questions that engage colleagues <br> Use follow-up questions to develop an organic discussion <br> Speak directly to your classmates (not the instructor) <br> Try your best to engage all of your classmates <br> Give your classmates “thinking time” after asking a question |
| Facilitation | Make eye contact with those around you <br> Paraphrase your own questions for further clarification, if needed <br> Listen actively and react to the words spoken by your classmates <br> Know when to “get out of the way” to let your classmates converse <br> Be ready to go “off script” when classmates head in a new direction <br> Be explicit when shifting the focus of the conversation |
