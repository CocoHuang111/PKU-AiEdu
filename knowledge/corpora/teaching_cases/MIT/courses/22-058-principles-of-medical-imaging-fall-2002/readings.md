## Readings

### Textbook

Cho, Z-H., J. Jones, and M. Singh. *Foundations of Medical Imaging*.

### Suggested Readings

There are a number of good texts on the subject and you may wish to consult some of the following:

Webb, S. *The Physics of Medical Imaging.*

Macovski, A. *Medical Imaging.*

Callaghan, P. *Principles of NMR Imaging.*

Blackledge, J. *Quantitative Coherent Imaging.*

Bushberg, J., J. Siebert, E. Leidholdt, and J. Boone. *The Essential Physics of Medical Imaging.*

Williamson, M. *Essentials of Ultrasound.*

Carlton, R., and A. Adler. *Principles of Radiographic Imaging.*

Barratt, H., and W. Swindell. *Radiological Imaging.*

Selman, J. *The Fundamentals of X-Ray and Radium Physics.*
