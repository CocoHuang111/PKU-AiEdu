## Readings

### Required Textbook

Mumford, David. *The Red Book of Varieties and Schemes.* Vol. 1358, *Lecture Notes in Mathematics.* New York: Springer-Verlag. ISBN: 354063293X.
Includes the Michigan Lectures (1974) on Curves and Their Jacobians.

### Suggested Textbooks

Shafarevich, I. R., and M. Reid. *Basic Algebraic Geometry I.* 2nd revised and expanded ed. Berlin; New York: Springer-Verlag Telos. ISBN: 0387548122.

Hartshorne, Robin. *Algebraic Geometry.* New York: Springer-Verlag, 1977. ISBN: 0387902449.

Eisenbud, David. *Commutative Algebra With a View Toward Algebraic Geometry.* Vol. 150, *Graduate Texts in Mathematics.* New York: Springer-Verlag, 1994. ISBN: 0387942696.

Atiyah, M. F., and I. G. MacDonald. *Introduction to Commutative Algebra.* Reading, Mass.: Addison Wesley Publishing Company, 1969. ISBN: 0201407515.
