### Course Overview

Experimental Projects I, II is the Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics’ undergraduate project laboratory course. Students take [16.621](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/16-621-experimental-projects-i-spring-2003) for a semester, and 16.622 for the following semester. The 16.621 / 16.622 syllabus below offers a detailed account of how these two courses are interrelated and structured.
