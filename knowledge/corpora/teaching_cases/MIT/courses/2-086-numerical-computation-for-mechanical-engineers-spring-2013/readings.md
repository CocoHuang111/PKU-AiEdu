## Readings

All readings are from the textbook: Masayuki Yano, et al. [*Math, Numerics, and Programming (for Mechanical Engineers)*](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-086-numerical-computation-for-mechanical-engineers-spring-2013/resources/mit2_086s13_textbook_pdf/). V2.1, August 2013. Files for each available unit are provided in the following table, courtesy of the authors.

Students should read all of the assignments for the unit before proceeding to the associated [Problem Sets](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-086-numerical-computation-for-mechanical-engineers-spring-2013/pages/assignments/) and the [MATLAB® Tutorials](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-086-numerical-computation-for-mechanical-engineers-spring-2013/pages/matlab-exercises/). In general, students should skip the advanced material in the textbook. This advanced material is indicated either by blue shading or by blue “Advanced Material” bookends.
