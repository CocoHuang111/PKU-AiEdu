## Readings Lecture 10: Transactions and Locking

In this class, we will begin our discussion of concurrency control and recovery. To prepare for this class, please read:

Franklin, Michael. “[Concurrency Control and Recovery (PDF)](https://courses.cs.washington.edu/courses/cse544/11wi/papers/franklin97.pdf)” *The Computer Science and Engineering Handbook*. 1997.

As you read, note any terms you are unfamiliar with and come to class prepared to ask questions!
