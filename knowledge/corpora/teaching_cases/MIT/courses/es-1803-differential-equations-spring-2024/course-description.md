## Course Description

This course, taught to students in MIT’s Experimental Study Group, is equivalent to the Mathematics Department’s [*18.03 Differential Equations*](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-03sc-differential-equations-fall-2011/). They satisfy the same prerequisites and are geared to preparing students for downstream science and engineering classes.

MIT’s [Experimental Study Group](https://esg.mit.edu/) (ESG) is one of MIT’s first-year learning communities. Small class sizes allow for teaching interactively and staying in close touch with what every student is finding easy or difficult.
