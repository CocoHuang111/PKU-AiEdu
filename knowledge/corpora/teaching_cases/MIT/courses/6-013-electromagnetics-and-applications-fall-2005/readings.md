## Readings

The table below provides the reading assignments for the course. The required readings are taken from the textbook *Electromagnetics and Applications* by D. H. Staelin. These readings are listed without an abbreviation in the table. The related reading sections for video demonstrations during the course are abbreviated as H/M. The entire contents of H/M can be found in Supplemental Resources > [Electromagnetic Fields and Energy](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-6-001-electromagnetic-fields-and-energy-spring-2008).

Staelin, David H. “Electromagnetics and Applications.” (Course notes, Massachusetts Institute of Technology, n.d.)

H/M = Haus, Hermann A., and James R. Melcher. *Electromagnetic Fields and Energy*. Englewood Cliffs, NJ: Prentice-Hall, 1989. ISBN: 9780132490207.
