### Design Project

For the final design project, there is a choice of two applications:

Tidal Turbine ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-23-hydrofoils-and-propellers-spring-2007/resources/tidal_turbine/))

SDV Propeller ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-23-hydrofoils-and-propellers-spring-2007/resources/sdv/))

The MPVL code associated with these projects is available on the [tools](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-23-hydrofoils-and-propellers-spring-2007/pages/tools/) page.
