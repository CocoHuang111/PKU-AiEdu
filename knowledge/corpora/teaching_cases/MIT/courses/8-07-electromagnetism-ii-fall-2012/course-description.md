## Course Description

This course is the second in a series on Electromagnetism beginning with Electromagnetism I ([8.02](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-02-physics-ii-electricity-and-magnetism-spring-2019/) or [8.022](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-022-physics-ii-electricity-and-magnetism-fall-2004/)). It is a survey of basic electromagnetic phenomena: electrostatics; magnetostatics; electromagnetic properties of matter; time-dependent electromagnetic fields; Maxwell’s equations; electromagnetic waves; emission, absorption, and scattering of radiation; and relativistic electrodynamics and mechanics.
