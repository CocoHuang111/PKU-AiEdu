## Assignments

### Weekly Reflections

As explained in the syllabus, the weekly reflections, corresponding to weeks 2–10, will be done as a discussion via the internal class website, and are due before class. For each week, reflect on one of that week’s papers, and specifically call out 5 things:

1. Problem being addressed
2. Contribution made
3. Evaluation used
4. Your opinion on whether its a meaningful contribution
5. The “secret terrible thing” about the paper
