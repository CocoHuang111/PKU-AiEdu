## Lists of Works Part I (Weeks 2–5)

*Please note*: You may be asked specifically about **bolded** works on exams.

### Week 2

1. Map of Italy, c. 1400
2. *Crucifix of Saint Damian*, late 12th century
3. Bonaventura Berlinghieri (active c. 1235–1244), ***St. Francis with Scenes from his Life***, 1235
4. Master of Santa Chiara, ***Saint Clare and Eight Scenes from her Life***, c. 1281–85
5. *Crucifix with St. Francis*, late 13th century
6. *Madonna dagli Occhi Grossi* (Madonna with the Big Eyes), 1220s
7. Duccio (active c. 1278–1318), *Maestà*, c. 1308–11: reconstruction of front full view + detail of *Madonna and Child*, reconstruction of back, **front panel**, ***Betrayal of Christ***, ***Crucifixion***, *Temptation of Christ*, *Announcement of the Death to the Virgin*
8. Cimabue, *Madonna Enthroned with Angels and Prophets*, 1280–1290
9. Giotto (c. 1267–1337), ***Madonna Enthroned***, c. 1310
10. Arena (Scrovegni) Chapel, c.1303–05
11. Giotto, frescoes in Arena (Scrovegni) Chapel, c.1303–05: *Lamentation*, *Meeting at the Golden Gate*, ***Kiss of Judas***, *Pact of Judas*, *Last Judgment*
