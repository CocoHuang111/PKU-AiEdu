## Lecture Notes

The course is organized so that each class revolves around a case discussion and a theory discussion. Copies of the slides for the theory discussions are available in the table below. Full citations for the case studies can be found on the [readings](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-840-special-seminar-in-marketing-marketing-management-spring-2004/pages/readings/) page.

In addition to participating actively during class, students should e-mail the instructors before the case discussion each week to point out three of the case’s major concepts.
