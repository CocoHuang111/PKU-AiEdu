## Course Description

This course examines the ethical dimensions of students’ professional lives, summer internships, and research projects. Students explore their own moral values through a range of ethical frameworks and questions. The course runs for ten weeks over the summer and concludes with a showcase of students’ independent projects in the first week of the fall term.
