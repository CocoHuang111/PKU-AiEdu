## Assignments

Due dates for the assignments and experiments can be found in the [calendar](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-02x-physics-ii-electricity-magnetism-with-an-experimental-focus-spring-2005/pages/syllabus/) on the syllabus page.

There are three types of assignments for this class: problem sets, [Mastering Physics](http://www.masteringphysics.com/) assignments (not available to OCW users) and experiments (located in the [labs](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-02x-physics-ii-electricity-magnetism-with-an-experimental-focus-spring-2005/pages/labs/) section) which must be completed and checked off during lab hours. Several of the problem sets contain questions about the experiments.
