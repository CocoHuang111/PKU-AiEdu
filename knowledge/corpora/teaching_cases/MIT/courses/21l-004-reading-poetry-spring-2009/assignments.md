| Week #3 (readings) | Read Wordsworth’s “My Heart Leaps Up,” and “The Solitary Reaper” and Yeats’ “Among School Children,” “The Lake Isle of Innisfree,” “Down By the Salley Gardens,” and “Sailing to Byzantium.” <br> Come to class with plentiful observations on “The World Is Too Much With Us.” Try to model what we did in class: read the poem many times, out loud too, turn it over and write down what you remember or what strikes you. You can return to the poem and keep writing. |
