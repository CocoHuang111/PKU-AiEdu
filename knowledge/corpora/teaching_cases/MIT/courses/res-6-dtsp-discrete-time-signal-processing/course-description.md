## Course Description

This page is the central hub for the third edition of *Discrete-Time Signal Processing* by Alan Oppenheim and Ron Schafer, available online for free. Here, you’ll find the complete third edition, along with a curated selection of related resources, including the following:

- Access to previous editions of the textbook, including the first and second editions, as well as the predecessor text, *Digital Signal Processing*
- Links to companion MIT OpenCourseWare and MITx courses by Alan Oppenheim

This comprehensive resource hub is designed to support both independent learners and educators, providing a rich collection of materials that cover the fundamentals and advanced topics in discrete-time signal processing, including signal representation, filter design, and multirate techniques.

This book was previously published by Pearson Education, Inc.
