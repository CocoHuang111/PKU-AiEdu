Goldsmith, Oliver. *The Vicar of Wakefield*. Hammondsworth, UK: Penguin Books, 1982, chapters 4-8. ISBN: 9780140431599. (The paperback is readily available or text is on line.)

Whisnant, David. *All That is Native and Fine*. Chapel Hill, NC: University of North Carolina Press, 1995. ISBN: 9780807841433.
