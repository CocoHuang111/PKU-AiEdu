## Assignments

The class has five [problem sets](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-642-topics-in-mathematics-with-applications-in-finance-fall-2024/pages/problem-sets/) covering math lecture topics.

There is also an optional [investment game](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-642-topics-in-mathematics-with-applications-in-finance-fall-2024/pages/investment-game/) which is ungraded.

Students must also participate in a mid-semester [group project](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-642-topics-in-mathematics-with-applications-in-finance-fall-2024/pages/group-project/) (a lecture note and in-class presentation).

Instead of a final exam, there is a [final paper](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-642-topics-in-mathematics-with-applications-in-finance-fall-2024/pages/final-paper/) on a math finance topic of the student’s choice.
