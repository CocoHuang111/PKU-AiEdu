## Readings

Class readings will be assigned from the following books:

[EE] Sullivan, W., E. Wicks, and J. Luxhoj. *Engineering Economy*. 12th ed. East Rutherford, NJ: Prentice-Hall, 2002. ISBN: 9780130673381.

[MS] Ashby, M. F. *Materials Selection in Mechanical Design*. 3rd ed. San Diego, CA: Elsevier, 2005. ISBN: 9780750661683.

[LC] Bauman, H., and A. Tillman. *Hitch Hiker’s Guide To LCA: An Orientation in Life Cycle Assessment Methodology and Application*. Lund, Sweden: Studentlitteratur AB, 2004. ISBN: 9789144023649.
