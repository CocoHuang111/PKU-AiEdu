## Lecture Notes

#### Lecture 1: Motivation, Intuition, and Examples

Outline: Motivation, definition, and intuition behind metric spaces. Redefining *18.100A Real Analysis* and *18.100P Real Analysis* in terms of metrics: open/closed sets, convergence, Cauchy sequences, and continuity.

[Lecture 1: Motivation, Intuition, and Examples (PDF)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-s190-introduction-to-metric-spaces-january-iap-2023/resources/mit18_s190iap23_lec1/)

[Lecture 1: Motivation, Intuition, and Examples (TEX)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-s190-introduction-to-metric-spaces-january-iap-2023/resources/mit18_s190iap23_lec1t/)

#### Lecture 2: General Theory

Outline: Some general theory of metric spaces regarding convergence, open and closed sets, continuity, and their relationship to one another.

[Lecture 2: General Theory (PDF)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-s190-introduction-to-metric-spaces-january-iap-2023/resources/mit18_s190iap23_lec2/)

[Lecture 2: General Theory (TEX)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-s190-introduction-to-metric-spaces-january-iap-2023/resources/mit18_s190iap23_lec2t/)

#### Lecture 3: Compact Sets in Rⁿ

Outline: Norms and analysis on finite sets (as motivation for compact sets). Topological compactness and sequential compactness. Today will focus purely on theorems regarding compact subsets of Rⁿ. The Heine-Borel theorem and the Bolzano-Weierstrass theorem.

[Lecture 3: Compact Sets in Rⁿ (PDF)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-s190-introduction-to-metric-spaces-january-iap-2023/resources/mit18_s190iap23_lec3/)

[Lecture 3: Compact Sets in Rⁿ (TEX)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-s190-introduction-to-metric-spaces-january-iap-2023/resources/mit18_s190iap23_lec3t/)

#### Lecture 4: Compact Metric Spaces

Outline: Compact sets on general metric spaces. Showing sequential compactness is equivalent to topological compactness, which is equivalent to being totally bounded and complete (on metric spaces).

[Lecture 4: Compact Metric Spaces (PDF)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-s190-introduction-to-metric-spaces-january-iap-2023/resources/mit18_s190iap23_lec4/)

[Lecture 4: Compact Metric Spaces (TEX)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-s190-introduction-to-metric-spaces-january-iap-2023/resources/mit18_s190iap23_lec4t/)

#### Lecture 5: Complete Metric Spaces
