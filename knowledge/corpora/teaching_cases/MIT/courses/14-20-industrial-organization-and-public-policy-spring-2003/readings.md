## Readings

The course will rely heavily on the required textbook: Jeffrey Church and Roger Ware, *Industrial Organization: A Strategic Approach* (Irwin McGraw-Hill, 2000, 1st edition). ISBN: 0071166459. The text is denoted on the reading list as “C&W.” Occasional additional readings (typically short news articles) may be handed out in the lecture preceding their discussion. Students are encouraged to scan the *Wall Street Journal, Financial Times*, business section of *The New York Times*, or *The Economist* magazine for articles relevant to the topics covered in the course.
