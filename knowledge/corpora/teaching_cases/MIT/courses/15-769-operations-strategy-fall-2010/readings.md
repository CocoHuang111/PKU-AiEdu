## Readings

The following readings are required for the class and have assigned [questions](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-769-operations-strategy-fall-2010/pages/study-questions/) for discussion. Assigned cases are also listed, and questions to guide the [case write-ups](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-769-operations-strategy-fall-2010/pages/assignments/) are also available. The following texts are required readings for the course:

[B&R] = Beckman, Sara, and Donald Rosenfield. *Operations Strategy: Competing in the 21st Century*. McGraw-Hill/Irwin, 2007. ISBN: 9780072500783.

[Fine] = Fine, Charles H. *Clockspeed: Winning Industry Control in the Age of Temporary Advantage*. Basic Books, 1999. ISBN: 9780738201535.
