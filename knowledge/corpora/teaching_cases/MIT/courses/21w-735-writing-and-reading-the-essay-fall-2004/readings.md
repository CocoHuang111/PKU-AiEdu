## Readings

### Required Texts

Klaus, C., et al. *In Depth: Essayists for Our Time.* 2nd ed. Princeton, NJ: Heinle, 1993. ISBN: 9780155001725.

Lopate, Phillip. *The Art of the Personal Essay*. Anchor; 1st Anchor Books Pbk. Ed edition, 1997. ISBN: 9780385423397.

Queenan, Joe, ed. *The Malcontents: The Best Bitter, Cynical, and Satirical Writing in the World.* Philadelphia, PA: Running Press Book Publishers, 2004. ISBN: 9780762416974.

Raymo, Chet. “An Ancient Brilliance.” In *The Soul of the Night: An Astronomical Pilgrimage*. Englewood Cliffs, N.J.: Prentice Hall, 1985, pp. 53-62. ISBN: 9780138228835.

Alexie, Sherman. “Captivity.” In *The Next American Essay.* Edited by John D’Agata. St. Paul, Minn.: Greywolf Press, 2002, pp. 63-69. ISBN: 9781555973759.

Photocopied essays provided by instructor.

### Optional Texts

Perelman, L., et al. *The Mayfield Handbook of Technical and Scientific Writing.* New York, NY: McGraw-Hill, 1997. ISBN: 9781559346474.
