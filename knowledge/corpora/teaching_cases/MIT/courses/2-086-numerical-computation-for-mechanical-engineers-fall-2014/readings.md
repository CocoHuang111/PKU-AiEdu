## Readings

The textbook for the course is the textbook Masayuki Yano, et al. [*Math, Numerics, and Programming (for Mechanical Engineers*](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-086-numerical-computation-for-mechanical-engineers-fall-2014/resources/mit2_086s13_textbook_pdf/)*)*. V2.1, August 2013. Unpublished manuscript. The textbook is referred to as MATLAB<sup>®</sup>: Textbook in the table below.

The less-is-more variant of the textbook is available in the [Nutshells & GUIs section](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-086-numerical-computation-for-mechanical-engineers-fall-2014/pages/nutshells-guis/): Masayuki Yano, et al. *Math, Numerics, & Programming for Mechanical Engineers… in a Nutshell*, V1.1, 2014. Unpublished manuscript.
