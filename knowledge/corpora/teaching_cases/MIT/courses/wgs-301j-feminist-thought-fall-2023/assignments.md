## Assignments

### Final Project

Parts of the final project:

- [**Keyword exercise**](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/wgs-301j-feminist-thought-fall-2023/pages/keyword-exercise/) **(due during session 5)**. See [Sample Keywords](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/wgs-301j-feminist-thought-fall-2023/pages/sample-keywords/).
- **Topic paragraph** **(due during session 5)**. This can be an extension of your keyword exercise or a completely different topic.
- [**Research plan**](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/wgs-301j-feminist-thought-fall-2023/pages/research-plan/) **(due during session 9)**: Provide a bibliography of your sources and three questions you will ask of your materials.
- **Preliminary outline** of arguments and evidence **(due during session 13)**: How will you organize your materials? What questions will you be asking? Use this outline to marshal your thoughts so you are ready to write the final paper. See [Some Thoughts on Writing Outlines](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/wgs-301j-feminist-thought-fall-2023/pages/some-thoughts-on-writing-outlines/).

[**Oral presentation**](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/wgs-301j-feminist-thought-fall-2023/pages/oral-presentation/) **(due during session 19 or 20)**

[**Final research paper**](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/wgs-301j-feminist-thought-fall-2023/pages/final-research-paper/) (2500–5000 words, 10–15 pp) **(due during session 21)**

**Op-ed**, letter to the editor, or other piece of public writing based on final paper or on a favorite reading in the course **(due during session 26)**
