## Readings

You will only be responsible for the material contained in lecture notes and other handouts. For additional reading and examples, you can use the following books:

- [GS] G. R. Grimmett and D. R. Stirzaker. *Probability and Random Processes.* Oxford University Press, 3rd edition, 2001. ISBN: 9780198572220.
- [C] E. Çinlar. *Probability and Stochastics.* Springer, 2011. ISBN: 9780387878584.
- [W] D. Williams. *Probability with Martingales.* Cambridge University Press, 1991. ISBN: 9780521406055.
- [BT] D. P. Bertsekas and J. N. Tsitsiklis. *Introduction to Probability.* 2nd Ed., Athena Scientific, 2008. ISBN: 9781886529236. [Chapter 1](http://athenasc.com/probbook.html) is available online.
