## Course Description

This four-part dinner series was created by Prof. Lawrence Susskind at the request of a group of students in MIT’s Department of Urban Studies and Planning ([DUSP](https://dusp.mit.edu/)) who felt the need for a graduate-level leadership course that would be attuned specifically to the needs of public sector professionals.

The trailer and session videos may be freely viewed, downloaded, and reused under MIT OpenCourseWare’s [Creative Commons BY-NC-SA](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/deed.en) license.
