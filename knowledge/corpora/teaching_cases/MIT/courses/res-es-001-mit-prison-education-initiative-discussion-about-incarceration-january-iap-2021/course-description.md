## Course Description

The [MIT Prison Education Initiative (PEI)](http://pei.mit.edu/) hosted this non-credit January IAP course to give the MIT community an opportunity to engage in discussions about mass incarceration and criminal justice reform. The Independent Activities Period (IAP) is a special four week term at MIT that runs from the first week of January until the end of the month.
