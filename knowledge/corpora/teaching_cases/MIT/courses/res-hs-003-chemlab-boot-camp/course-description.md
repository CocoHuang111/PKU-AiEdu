## Course Description

Each year, groups of MIT freshmen are introduced to MIT’s laboratory environment through a four-week intensive January course called [*5.301 Chemistry Lab Techniques*](http://ocw.mit.edu/courses/chemistry/5-301-chemistry-laboratory-techniques-january-iap-2012/). The stakes are high—students who pass the class are guaranteed a job in an MIT research lab.

OpenCourseWare documented the experience of 14 students who took this course in January 2012. Follow their journey over 11 episodes and watch as they struggled with, but ultimately mastered, the techniques needed to be successful in an MIT chemistry lab.
