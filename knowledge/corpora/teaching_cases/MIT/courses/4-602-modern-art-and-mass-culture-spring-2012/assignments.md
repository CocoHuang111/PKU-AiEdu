| ASSIGNMENTS | FILES |

| Paper 4 <br> Take the previous paper that received the lowest grade, conduct further research based on preliminary questions and comments, and revise, rewrite, and resubmit it. | [Paper 4 Guidelines (PDF)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/4-602-modern-art-and-mass-culture-spring-2012/resources/mit4_602s12_paper4/) |
