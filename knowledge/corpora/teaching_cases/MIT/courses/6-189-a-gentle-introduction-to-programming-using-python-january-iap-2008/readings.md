## Readings

### Textbook

The course textbook is [*How to Think Like a Computer Scientist*](http://www.greenteapress.com/thinkpython/thinkCSpy/), through Chapter 12 (classes and objects). Links to individual chapters can be found [here](http://www.greenteapress.com/thinkpython/thinkCSpy/html/).

The lectures stick with the order of the textbook for the most part, but not always.

### Links

The [Python Quick Reference (2.4)](http://rgruet.free.fr/PQR24/PQR2.4.html) lists and explains all of Python’s built-in functions and behavior.
