# Multiple Modalities for Different Learners

SCM.260/SCM.266 Management (Course 15)           Faculty/Instructor  Chris Caplice    Digital Innovations & Tools  Active Learning Online Assessments & Rapid Feedback Lightboard

Professor Chris Caplice delivers content in different modalities in order to reach a broad range of learners. Caplice also begins each class with a 5-15 minute lightboard video, an effective way to talk about a quantitative topic qualitatively. He uses interactive polling to gauge how the class is doing as well as to encourage introverted or reticent students to participate.

Winner of the [2018 Teaching With Digital Technology Award](https://openlearning.mit.edu/campus/teaching-digital-technology-awards/2018-awards), Prof Caplice tells the story of his teaching innovations in this video.
