# Designing and Assembling Printed Circuit Boards for Impactful Projects

6.9000 Electrical Engineering & Computer Science (Course 6)     ![Headshot of Joel Voldman](https://openlearning.mit.edu/sites/default/files/styles/event_news_detail/public/residential-innovations/Joel_Voldman_05_22_23.00_02_08_26.Still001.png?h=72dcaf05&itok=nKuIptA0)       Faculty/Instructor  Joel Voldman    Digital Innovations & Tools  Active Learning

In 6.9000, *Engineering for Impact*, Professor Joel Voldman helps students to design and assemble printed circuit boards for use in real project settings. The printed circuit boards are designed, then manufactured, then put together by students, who are often excited to see their designs turn into working products with real impact.
