# Task-Centered Learning: Intro to EECS Robotics

6.01 Electrical Engineering & Computer Science (Course 6)           Faculty/Instructor  Dennis Freeman    Digital Innovations & Tools  Active Learning

Prof. Freeman uses task-centered learning which draws from both lecture format and project-based learning. Class begins with a challenge. As students struggle to complete the challenge, they become receptive to the instruction they need. When students use their newly gained knowledge to complete the challenge, there is excitement and a sense of accomplishment.
