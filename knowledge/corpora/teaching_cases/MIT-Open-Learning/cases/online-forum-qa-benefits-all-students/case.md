# Online Forum for Q&A Benefits All Students

8.033, 8.233 Physics (Course 8)           Faculty/Instructor  Tracy Slatyer    Digital Innovations & Tools  Blended Learning & Flipped Classroom Online Assessments & Rapid Feedback Piazza

Prof Tracy Slatyer uses the online forum Piazza to address and answer student questions between classes. With an interface that allows a student to ask a question seen by the rest of the class, Prof Slatyer is able to more efficiently clear up misunderstandings that might affect more than one student. Questions can be asked anonymously.
