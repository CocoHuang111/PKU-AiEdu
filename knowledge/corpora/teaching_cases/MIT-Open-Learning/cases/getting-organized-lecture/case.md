# Getting Organized for Lecture

5.111 Chemistry (Course 5)           Faculty/Instructor  Brett McGuire

For 5.111, Professor Brett McGuire organized his lecture using a particular typeset, spacing, and color-coding, and by providing a detailed script. The organized lecture slides and scripts were used by students for review.
