# Using Interactive Graphics and Code to Teach Robotics

6.8210 & 6.4210 Electrical Engineering & Computer Science (Course 6)     ![Headshot of Russell Tedrake](https://openlearning.mit.edu/sites/default/files/styles/event_news_detail/public/residential-innovations/Russ_06_14_23.00_01_22_12.Still001.png?h=ac08ee0f&itok=gFUPlDkT)       Faculty/Instructor  Russell Tedrake    Digital Innovations & Tools  Active Learning Blended Learning & Flipped Classroom Flexible Course Delivery Simulations & Visualizations

Professor Russell Tedrake brings together different technologies to help students grasp and apply concepts in 6.8210 & 6.4210, *Underactuated Robotics* & *Robotic Manipulation*. Course notes are open and online, including videos, animations, codes, simulations, and plots that are interactive. Lectures are posted to YouTube, and the balance of theory and demonstration help make the knowledge accessible and understandable to students.
