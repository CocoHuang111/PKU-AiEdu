# Curricular Modularity in Effective Field Theory

8.EFT Physics (Course 8)           Faculty/Instructor  Iain Stewart    Digital Innovations & Tools  Flexible Course Delivery

Residential MITx course content delivery provides “anytime anywhere” access, especially helpful for graduate subjects which are not taught every year. In 8.EFT, Prof. Iain Stewart divided his course into modules so that when Ph.D. students need to understand a concept in the course, they can directly access the corresponding module, creating more efficient use of their and Prof. Stewart’s time.
