# Collaborative Design in "Principals & Practices in Assistive Technology"

6.811/2.78/HST.420/3.008 Mechanical Engineering (Course 2)           Faculty/Instructor  Julie Greenberg    Digital Innovations & Tools  Active Learning

In this video, Dr. Julie Greenberg describes an innovative pedagogy of “designer/co-designer,” where students (designers) work closely in partnership with differently-abled clients (co-designers). This collaborative approach honors the contribution of the co-designer by highlighting his or her unique perspective and gives students the opportunity to create customized solutions that reflect genuine needs. Such an approach is transferable to other client-based disciplines and many entrepreneurial endeavors.

For additional detailed information about specific projects in Dr. Greenberg's class, as well as a more in-depth description of classroom activities, please view the [full video](https://youtu.be/xTCPn47xkFk?t=871) of her presentation.
