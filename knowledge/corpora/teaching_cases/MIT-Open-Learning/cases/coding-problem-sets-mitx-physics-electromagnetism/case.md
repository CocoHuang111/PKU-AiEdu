# Coding Problem Sets into MITx: Physics Electromagnetism

8.02 Physics (Course 8)     ![8.02](https://openlearning.mit.edu/sites/default/files/styles/event_news_detail/public/residential-innovations/8.02.png?h=dd78a3b2&itok=g68E-c6R)       Faculty/Instructor  Physics Faculty    Digital Innovations & Tools  Online Assessments & Rapid Feedback

"*8.02 TEAL+x: Student Say Yes to MITx in 8.02 TEAL*". In this Faculty Newsletter article, Prof. Belcher and Dr. Rayyan explain how homework problems are coded into the MITx platform so that students can immediately check their intermediate and final answers -- with a green check mark if correct or a red cross if incorrect. 92% of students rated the checkable answers on MITx as helpful, enabling them to:

1. learn from their mistakes
2. reduce stress
3. and raise self-confidence.
