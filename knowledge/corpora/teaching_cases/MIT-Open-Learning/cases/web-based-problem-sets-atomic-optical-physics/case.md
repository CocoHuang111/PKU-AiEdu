# Web-based Problem Sets in Atomic & Optical Physics

8.421 Physics (Course 8)     ![Wolfgang Ketterle pic 5](https://openlearning.mit.edu/sites/default/files/styles/event_news_detail/public/residential-innovations/Ketterle11.jpg?itok=V4cqt5j4)       Faculty/Instructor  Wolfgang Ketterle    Digital Innovations & Tools  Adaptive Hinting

Professor Ketterle collaborated with Professor Isaac Chuang to use the MITx platform to offer residential students interactive web-based problems. MITx allowed students to ask for advice when they got stuck on a problem. When they submitted the wrong answer, they received feedback immediately and could attempt the problem again.
