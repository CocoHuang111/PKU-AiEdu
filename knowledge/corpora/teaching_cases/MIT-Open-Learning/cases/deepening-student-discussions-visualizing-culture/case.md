# Deepening Student Discussions in Visualizing Culture

21G.027 Global Languages (Course 21G)     ![VizCultures](https://openlearning.mit.edu/sites/default/files/styles/event_news_detail/public/residential-innovations/VizCultures.jpg?h=e41f7a06&itok=R65ctQQi)       Faculty/Instructor  Shigeru Miyagawa    Digital Innovations & Tools  Blended Learning & Flipped Classroom

Prof. Miyagawa had the students in his residential class watch the video lectures and quizzes from his MOOC of the same material. Flipping the classroom in this way allowed Miyagawa to delve deeper into the subject matter. Students learned from each other and used their knowledge to raise further issues, which reinforced their learning. Prof Miyagawa interviewed on OCW: [https://bit.ly/2JVvDqJ](https://bit.ly/2JVvDqJ).
