# Flexible Access to Curricula: Mechanical Behavior of Materials

3.032 Materials Science and Engineering (Course 3)           Faculty/Instructor  Lorna Gibson    Digital Innovations & Tools  Blended Learning & Flipped Classroom

When Prof. Gibson flipped her class the online problem sets were now in sync with the lecture material. The students would watch a set of videos, and then attend a face-to-face recitation with Gibson. Her students also liked the flexible access to videos and other online material.
