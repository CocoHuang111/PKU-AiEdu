# Leveraging Best Practices from Remote Teaching for On-Campus Education

Multiple Subjects/Disciplines Office of Digital Learning     ![Students walking across the crosswalk, with MIT Building 7 in the background.](https://openlearning.mit.edu/sites/default/files/styles/event_news_detail/public/residential-innovations/back2campus.png?itok=Z4mjxcqA)       Faculty/Instructor  MIT Ad Hoc Committee    Digital Innovations & Tools  Active Learning Blended Learning & Flipped Classroom Flexible Course Delivery Remote Teaching

“What are the things that we learned how to do during the pandemic that faculty and instructors across MIT are now, already, building into their on-campus teaching?” Faculty and instructors across MIT engaged with this question and shared practices with the Ad Hoc Committee on Leveraging Best Practices from Remote Teaching for On-Campus Education. The [committee report](https://openlearning.mit.edu/sites/default/files/inline-files/LeveragingBestPracticesReportAug9.pdf) shares the themes and findings, a list of best practices, as well as recommendations. A goal of the report is to share what faculty and instructors across MIT are doing in order to learn from each other and find advice for teaching and supporting student learning.
