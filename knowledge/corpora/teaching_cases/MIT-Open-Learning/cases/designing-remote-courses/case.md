# Designing Remote Courses

Office of Digital Learning     ![empty classroom](https://openlearning.mit.edu/sites/default/files/styles/event_news_detail/public/residential-innovations/pexels-pixabay-207691.jpg?itok=4omxz47Z)       Faculty/Instructor  Lauren Totino Aaron Kessler    Digital Innovations & Tools  Remote Teaching

[This document](https://openlearning.mit.edu/sites/default/files/inline-files/DesigningRemoteCourses.pdf) is a resource aimed towards supporting instructors with effective remote teaching and learning during the Spring 2022 semester. Its focus is on developing courses with available technology such as Canvas and is grounded in research and supported by evidence from MIT instructors.
