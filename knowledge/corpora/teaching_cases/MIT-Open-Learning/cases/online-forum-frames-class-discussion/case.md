# Online Forum Frames In-class Discussion

11.124 Urban Studies and Planning (Course 11)           Faculty/Instructor  Eric Klopfer Wendy Huang    Digital Innovations & Tools  Active Learning Blended Learning & Flipped Classroom

Eric Klopfer, Wendy Huang, and their associates have their students use an online forum to discuss an assigned article prior to the in-class discussion. The online discussion helps to better frame and focus the in-class discussion.

Learn more about [Klopfer's teaching insights on OCW](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/11-124-introduction-to-education-looking-forward-and-looking-back-on-education-fall-2011/pages/instructor-insights/building-discussion-through-online-and-in-class-activities/).
