# Lightboard Use in Physics

8 Physics (Course 8)           Faculty/Instructor  Peter Dourmashkin    Digital Innovations & Tools  Lightboard

Physics instructor Peter Dourmashkin explains the light board and shares video examples from various disciplines. A lighted glass plane which leverages one’s natural mode of lecturing: the lightboard is a transparent vertical surface. The instructor is videotaped while writing on the lightboard which allows him/her to look at the material while also making virtual eye contact with the viewer.
