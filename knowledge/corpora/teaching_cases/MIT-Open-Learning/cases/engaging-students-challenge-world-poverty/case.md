# Engaging Students: Challenge of World Poverty

14.73 Economics (Course 14)     ![Duflo](https://openlearning.mit.edu/sites/default/files/styles/event_news_detail/public/residential-innovations/Duflo.jpg?h=e69dceb8&itok=2dRjoWoj)       Faculty/Instructor  Esther Duflo Abhijit Banerjee    Digital Innovations & Tools  Blended Learning & Flipped Classroom

This OCW course on The Challenges of World Poverty, taught by Profs. Esther Duflo & Abhijit Banerjee employs a “flipped” model, where students are expected to watch the lectures at home on the edX platform as well as answer "finger exercises" and homework questions online. This frees up class time for more interactive activities.
