# Online Application Lets Students Hear & Play: Fundamentals of Music

21M.051 Music and Theater Arts (Course 21M)           Faculty/Instructor  Michael Scott Cuthbert    Digital Innovations & Tools  Active Learning

Music Professor Michael Cuthbert uses a digital application Artusi which requires students to not only know the right intervals and chords but to hear and play them as well. Students learn in a way that is more efficient and more musical than traditional paper-based exercises.
