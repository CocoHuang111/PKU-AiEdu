# Gauging Student Comprehension: Discrete-Time Signal Processing

6.341 Electrical Engineering & Computer Science (Course 6)           Faculty/Instructor  Alan Oppenheim    Digital Innovations & Tools  Online Assessments & Rapid Feedback

Prof. Oppenheim uses clickers during class to provide anonymous, instantaneous feedback for himself and the students as to how they're relating to and understanding new material.

Homework problems are online on MITx. The students receive immediate feedback as to whether their answers are correct or not.
