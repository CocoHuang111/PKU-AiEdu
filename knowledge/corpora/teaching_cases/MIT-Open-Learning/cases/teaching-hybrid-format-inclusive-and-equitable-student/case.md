# Teaching in Hybrid Format for an Inclusive and Equitable Student Experience

MAS.858 Media Arts and Sciences (Course MAS)     ![Headshot of Danielle Wood](https://openlearning.mit.edu/sites/default/files/styles/event_news_detail/public/residential-innovations/Danielle_06_13_23.00_01_40_17.Still001.png?h=3f71223a&itok=i5T8PtmG)       Faculty/Instructor  Danielle Wood    Digital Innovations & Tools  Active Learning Blended Learning & Flipped Classroom Flexible Course Delivery Remote Teaching

Feeling a sense of belonging is a key aspect of learning. Professor Danielle Wood fosters community in the classroom by ensuring that both virtual and in-person participants engage in course activities. With a Zoom and iPad set up, students can engage with each other and the instructor. This hybrid course setup makes MAS.858, *Space Technology for the Development Leader*, more inclusive and equitable for students with different backgrounds and responsibilities.
