# Profile: Lily Pérez ’24, Writing Partner

Tuesday, May 14, 2024                ![Headshot of Lily Perez, a writing partner at the Poorvu Center](https://poorvucenter.yale.edu/sites/default/files/styles/max_width_320/public/2025-03/lily-perez-headshot-20250326.jpg?itok=Jtkvf28q)

Yale College senior Lily Pérez ’24 shares advice for incoming students:

“Keep an eye out for lectures, college teas, performances, and other events — you never know what world-famous speaker will be coming to campus. This is something so many Yale students take for granted. And there’s almost always free food!”

For the past two years, Lily has supported the academic success of peers through the Poorvu Center’s [Yale College Writing Center](https://poorvucenter.yale.edu/undergraduates/writing-center), as both a Residential College Writing Tutor and Weekly Writing Partner.

Congratulations to Lily and the Yale Class of 2024!
