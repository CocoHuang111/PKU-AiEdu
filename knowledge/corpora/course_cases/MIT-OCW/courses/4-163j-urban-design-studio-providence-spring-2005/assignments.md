## Assignments

<table><tr><th>week #</th><th>topics</th><th>assignments</th></tr><tr><td colspan="3">Part I: Background Research</td></tr><tr><td>1</td><td>Briefing and Reconnaissance</td><td></td></tr><tr><td>2</td><td>Documentation and Analysis</td><td>Document the Downtown Providence Plan <br> (a) Visit Providence, walk the streets and document the city with photographs, drawings, and maps; and <br> (b) Create an accurate, current plan of Central Providence by modifying an AutoCAD base map.</td></tr><tr><td>3</td><td>Interpretive Analysis</td><td>Analyze Central Providence <br> Using standard urban design analysis techniques, analyze the central city in terms of <br> (a) Building types <br> (b) Blocks (size, density, etc.) <br> (c) Streets (width, section, height of adjacent buildings, density of adjacent buildings, architecture, regularity, etc.) <br> (d) Open spaces (size, location, shape, density, etc.) <br> (e) Use <br> (f) Circulation <br> (g) Etc. <br> Also identify Central Providence’s positive characteristics and problematic issues.</td></tr><tr><td colspan="3">Part II: Urban Design Concept Development</td></tr><tr><td>4</td><td>Alternative Concepts</td><td>Develop a series of alternate urban design concepts based on your documentation and analysis. These concepts should be represented in plan form.</td></tr><tr><td>5-9</td><td>Urban Design: Public Spaces, Building Typologies and Plots, Facades and Streets, Urban Plan and Massing and Program</td><td>Pick a preferred scheme from the alternatives developed in Week 4. Further develop the scheme especially in terms of its <br> (a) Public Spaces (plan, section, elevation, perspective); <br> (b) Block structure including lots and functional uses (plan); <br> (c) Facades (elevation, section); <br> (d) Streets (plan, section, elevation, perspective); <br> (e) Urban Plan (plan); <br> (f) Massing (plan, elevation, perspective); and <br> (g) Program (plan).</td></tr><tr><td colspan="3">Part III: Recommendations / Documentation</td></tr><tr><td>10</td><td>Illustrative Design and Guidelines: Urban Guidelines, Streets, Spaces</td><td>Identify a specific precinct or local area to develop in more detail. Develop a set of urban design guidelines for this area. Illustrate the streets and spaces of this precinct using plans, sections, elevations, and perspective drawings.</td></tr><tr><td>11-14</td><td>Documentation and Presentation</td><td>Bring the work of the semester together into a final presentation document and present the work to a panel of faculty, outside reviewers and a representative from the City of Providence.</td></tr></table>
