## Readings

In addition to the readings listed below for the Spring 2004 version of the course, you may also download Prof. Shankar Raman’s syllabus from his version of the course ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21l-009-shakespeare-spring-2004/resources/21l009_f03-1/)). The syllabus includes his reading list.

The following texts are used in the course. See the [assignments section](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21l-009-shakespeare-spring-2004/pages/assignments/) for details on how the readings are timed with the essays and other requirements of the course. Links to downloadable versions of each text are provided below.
