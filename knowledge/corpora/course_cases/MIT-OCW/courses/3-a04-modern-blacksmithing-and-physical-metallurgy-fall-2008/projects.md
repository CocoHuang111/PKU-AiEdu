### Bottle Opener

[The first student project assignment was to make a bottle opener.](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-a04-modern-blacksmithing-and-physical-metallurgy-fall-2008/pages/projects/5bd69532bd697e0f7298167733dbd711_048.jpg) [Start with a piece of bar stock 1/4x1, and put a notch in it 1.5 from the end.](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-a04-modern-blacksmithing-and-physical-metallurgy-fall-2008/pages/projects/7a5c04b44980db35be21a4120e54bcb8_049.jpg) [Use a spring fuller to neck the material at that point down to 0.5 wide.](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-a04-modern-blacksmithing-and-physical-metallurgy-fall-2008/pages/projects/5eda681398f26b45c7323130964b3ab3_050.jpg) [Use a spring fuller to neck the material at that point down to 0.5 wide.](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-a04-modern-blacksmithing-and-physical-metallurgy-fall-2008/pages/projects/62e49412759e8ea3f85e0ede71487741_051.jpg) [Hammer a taper into what will be the handle of your bottle opener, up to the area that was just fullered.](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-a04-modern-blacksmithing-and-physical-metallurgy-fall-2008/pages/projects/9a6cef3234b6dc403738c68a0dd52077_052.jpg)   The first student project assignment was to make a bottle opener. Start with a piece of bar stock 1/4"x1", and put a notch in it 1.5" from the end. This will become the neck between the handle and the opener. Use a spring fuller to neck the material at that point down to 0.5" wide. Hammer a taper into what will be the handle of your bottle opener, up to the area that was just fullered.
