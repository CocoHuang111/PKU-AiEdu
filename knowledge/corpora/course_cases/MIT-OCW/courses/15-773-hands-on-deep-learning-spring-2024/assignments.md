### Project Assignment

Student teams presented their projects on the final day of class.

#### Project Proposal

You will submit a 1-page proposal for your final project. The proposal will clearly describe the problem (both from a business and technical perspective), the dataset, and your proposed approach(es).

Each group only needs to submit one proposal by one person.

#### Project Report

Please submit a PDF of your project report, which should include a link to your Colab notebook.

Your report should be between 5 and 10 pages (including appendices) with sections for Problem Description, Approach, Results, and Lessons Learned.

#### Project Title and Abstract

Please submit a **title** and a few-sentence **abstract** of your final project. The abstracts will be compiled and sent out to all students for voting before the final class meeting.

The top 8 teams will be selected to present for 8 minutes each, plus 1 minute of questions. **The winning teams will be announced in real time during the final day of class, so all teams should come prepared to present.**
