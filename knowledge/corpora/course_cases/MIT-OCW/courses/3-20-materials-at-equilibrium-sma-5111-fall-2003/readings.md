## Readings

### Course Texts

Zemansky, Mark, and Richard Dittman. *Heat and Thermodynamics*. 7th ed. McGraw-Hill Publishers.

McQuarrie, Donald A. *Statistical Thermodynamics.*

Stokes, R. J., and D. F. Evans. *Fundamentals of Interfacial Engineering*. Wiley VCH.

### Other Interesting Texts

Hillert, M. *Phase Equilibria, Phase Diagrams and Phase Transformations: Their Thermodynamic Basis.*

Gaskel, D. *Introduction to Metallurgical Thermodynamics.*

———. *Introduction to the Thermodynamics of Materials.*

DeHoff, R. T. *Thermodynamics in Materials Science.*

Kubaschewsky, O. *Materials Thermochemistry*.

Lupis, C. H. P. *Chemical Thermodynamics*.

Gordon. *Principles of Phase Diagrams in Material Systems*.

Callen, H. B. *Thermodynamics and an Introduction to Thermostatistics*.

Swalin, R. A. Chapter 7, “A Model Approach to Alloys.” in *Thermodynamics of Solids*. Hoboken NJ: Wiley-Interscience, 1972.
