## Syllabus

### Course Meeting Times

Lectures: 2 sessions / week, 1.5 hours / session

### Prerequisites

*Quasi-balanced Circulations in Oceans and Atmospheres* ([12.803](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/12-803-quasi-balanced-circulations-in-oceans-and-atmospheres-fall-2009)) or *Dynamics of the Atmosphere* ([12.810](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/12-810-dynamics-of-the-atmosphere-spring-2008)).

### Assigned Readings

Paluch, Ilga. “[The Entrainment Mechanism in Colorado Cumuli](http://dx.doi.org/10.1175/1520-0469%281979%29036%3C2467:TEMICC%3E2.0.CO;2).” *Journal of the Atmospheric Science* 36, no. 12 (1979): 2467–78.

Romps, David, and Zhiming Kuang. “[Do Undiluted Convective Plumes Exist in the Upper Tropical Troposphere?](http://dx.doi.org/10.1175/2009JAS3184.1)” *Journal of the Atmospheric Science* 67, no. 2 (2010): 468–84.

### Topics

1. Radiative-Convective Equilibrium
2. The Zonally-Averaged Circulation
3. Asymmetric Steady Circulations
4. Interannual Fluctuations of the Walker Circulation - ENSO
5. Intraseasonal Oscillations
6. Higher Frequency Disturbances
7. Tropical Cyclones

### Grading

The course grade is based upon problem sets, a term paper, and class participation.
