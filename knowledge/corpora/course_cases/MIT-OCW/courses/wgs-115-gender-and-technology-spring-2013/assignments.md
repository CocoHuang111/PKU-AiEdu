## Assignments

### [Paper 1](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/wgs-115-gender-and-technology-spring-2013/pages/assignments/paper-1/)—Due Between Sessions 2 and 3

The first assignment is a short paper on a gendered technology, with reference to at least one class text.

### [Paper 2](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/wgs-115-gender-and-technology-spring-2013/pages/assignments/paper-2/)—Due Session 6

Write a short critical essay of 3–5 pages in response to your chosen topic, with reference to at least two class texts.

### [Final Paper](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/wgs-115-gender-and-technology-spring-2013/pages/assignments/final-paper/)—Due Session 12

There are two options for the final paper, an independent research project or a series of essays answering specific questions.[https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/wgs-115-gender-and-technology-spring-2013/pages/assignments/final-paper/](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/wgs-115-gender-and-technology-spring-2013/pages/assignments/final-paper/)
