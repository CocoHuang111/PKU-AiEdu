## Calendar

| WEEK # | TOPICS |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Introduction: Defining happiness <br> Do pre-class questionnaire <br> Do Who Am I exercise—discuss why you are taking this class <br> Look at different definitions of happiness |
| 2 | Overview of positive psychology <br> What is it and how does it differ from traditional psychology? <br> What are the benefits and drawbacks of this approach? <br> How does it work in therapy? |
| 3 | Happiness vs. pleasure <br> What is happiness? <br> How is it measured? <br> How does it differ from pleasure? |
| 4 | Cognitive styles <br> Optimism & pessimism—what is the difference? <br> How do they work in practice? <br> Is there such a thing as realistic optimism? Realistic pessimism? |
| 5 | Strengths <br> What are our strengths? <br> How can we use them and in what capacities? <br> What are their limitations? <br> Guest speaker: Tamara Menghi, Associate Director, Employer Relations and Career Programs, <br> [*MIT Global Education & Career Development*](http://gecd.mit.edu/) |
| 6 | Unhappiness, sadness and depression <br> What is depression and how does it differ from sadness or unhappiness—how does it relate to suffering? <br> Is there value in suffering? <br> How can we recognize and treat depression? |
| 7 | Happiness in relationships <br> Do friendship maps exercise <br> Discuss what makes for a good romantic relationship <br> Discuss healthy relationships with parents |
| 8 | Wellness and flow <br> Class exercises to include yoga and meditation |
| 9 | Happiness and diversity (cultural and religious) <br> Investigates how different cultures and generations define happiness <br> Viewing <br> Video clips from interviews with people from a different culture |
| 10 | Happiness and decision making <br> Guest speaker: Dorise Gruber, Assistant Director for Freshman Affairs, <br> [*Boston University School of Management*](http://management.bu.edu/) |
| 11 | Finding meaning in our lives |
| 12 | Open topic (to be decided by students), evaluations and wrap up |
