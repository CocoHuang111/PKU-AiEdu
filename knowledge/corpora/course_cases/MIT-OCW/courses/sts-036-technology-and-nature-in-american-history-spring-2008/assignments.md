## Assignments

Students are required to complete two types of writing assignments during the term: six reading response papers and three formal papers. See the [syllabus](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/sts-036-technology-and-nature-in-american-history-spring-2008/pages/syllabus/) for more details.

### Reading Response Papers

Guidelines ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/sts-036-technology-and-nature-in-american-history-spring-2008/resources/response_guide/))

Sample ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/sts-036-technology-and-nature-in-american-history-spring-2008/resources/response_sample/))

#### Response Paper Topics

- Week 2 - Cronon, *Changes in the Land*
- Week 3 - Basso, *Wisdom Sits in Places*
- Week 5 - Thoreau, *Walden*
- Week 7 - Cronon, *Nature’s Metropolis*
- Week 8 - Solnit, *River of Shadows*
- Week 10 - Worster, *Dust Bowl*
