## Readings

The following readings are assigned for the first few weeks of class.

Franklin, Gene F., J. David Powell, and Michael L. Workman. *Digital Control of Dynamic Systems*. 3rd ed. Upper Saddle River, NJ: Prentice Hall, 1997. ISBN: 9780201820546.

- Week 1: Chapter 1, Chapter 2, Chapter 3

- Week 2: Chapter 4 (starred sections optional)

- Week 4: Section 5.1, 5.2, and 5.3

- Week 5: Section 6.1, 6.2, 7.1, 7.2, and 7.3

Also for Week 5: Oppenheim, A. V., and A. S. Willsky. *Signals and Systems*. 2nd ed. Upper Saddle River, NJ: Prentice Hall, 1996, chapter 7. ISBN: 9780138147570.
