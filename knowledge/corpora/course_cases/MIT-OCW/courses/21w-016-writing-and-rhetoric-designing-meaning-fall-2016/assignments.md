## Assignments

### Essays

**[Essay 1: Speech Analysis](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21w-016-writing-and-rhetoric-designing-meaning-fall-2016/pages/assignments/essay-1/)**

Draft due: One day before Session 8

Revision due: Two days after Session 10

Length: 1,000 words

Purpose: To learn to analyze features of public argument, and to consider your own claims and appeals to a public audience.

*Example student work:*

[The Next Four Years of Values (PDF)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21w-016-writing-and-rhetoric-designing-meaning-fall-2016/resources/mit21w_016f16_essay1/) - Courtesy of an MIT student and used with permission.

**[Essay 2: Debate Analysis](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21w-016-writing-and-rhetoric-designing-meaning-fall-2016/pages/assignments/essay-2/)**

Draft due: Three days after Session 16

Revision due: Two days after Session 20

Length: 1,250 words

Purpose: To learn to analyze arguments on both sides of a debate in relation to each other, and to consider your own claims and appeals to a public audience.

*Example student work:*

[Analysis of Trump v. Clinton debate on Gun Control (PDF)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21w-016-writing-and-rhetoric-designing-meaning-fall-2016/resources/mit21w_016f16_essay2/) - Courtesy of an MIT student and used with permission.

**[Essay 3: Digital Rhetoric Analysis](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21w-016-writing-and-rhetoric-designing-meaning-fall-2016/pages/assignments/essay-3/)**

Draft due: One day before Session 24

Oral presentation of your own MIT homepage image: in class on Session 26, ~10 minutes

Revision due: Last day of class, Session 27

Length: 1,750 words

*Example student work:*

[A Community of a Thousand Influences (PDF)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21w-016-writing-and-rhetoric-designing-meaning-fall-2016/resources/mit21w_016f16_essay3/) - Courtesy of an MIT student and used with permission.
