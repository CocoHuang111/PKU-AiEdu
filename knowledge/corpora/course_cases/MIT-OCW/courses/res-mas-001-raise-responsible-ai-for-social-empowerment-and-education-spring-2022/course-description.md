## Course Description

RAISE (Responsible AI for Social Empowerment and Education) is a new MIT-wide initiative headquartered in the [MIT Media Lab](https://www.media.mit.edu/) and in collaboration with the [MIT Schwarzman College of Computing](https://computing.mit.edu/) and [MIT Open Learning](https://openlearning.mit.edu/). MIT researchers continually develop [curriculum modules and associated teaching materials](https://raise.mit.edu/resources/) that are available to all K-12 educators for free under a Creative Commons license.
