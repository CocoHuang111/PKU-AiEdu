## Calendar

The class meets twice a week, on Tuesday and on Fridays. The Tuesday class is usually seminar-style with students presenting and discussing papers assigned by the upcoming guest speaker. A [full list of readings](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-s898-climate-change-seminar-fall-2019/pages/readings/) is available. The Friday class features guest speakers.
