## Readings

Adams, Ross Exo, “[Natura Urbans, Natura Urbanata: Ecological Urbanism, Circulation, and the Immunization of Nature](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/275321226_Natura_Urbans_Natura_Urbanata_Ecological_Urbanism_Circulation_and_the_Immunization_of_Nature),” *Environment and Planning D: Society and Space* 32 (2014): 12–29.

Aristotle, [*Politics*. Book 7, chapters 4–7](https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/politics.7.seven.html).

Barles, Sabine, and Laurence Lestel, [“The Nitrogen Question. Urbanization, Industrialization, and River Quality in Paris 1830–1939,”](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0096144207301421) *Journal of Urban History* 33, no. 5 (2007): 794–812.

Batty, Michael, “The Unpredictable City,” in [*The Computable City: Histories, Technologies, Stories, Predictions*](https://direct.mit.edu/books/monograph/5756/The-Computable-CityHistories-Technologies-Stories) (Cambridge, MA: The MIT Press, 2024).

Benevolo, Leonardo, “Roman Urbanism,” in [*The Origins of Modern Town Planning*](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262520188/the-origins-of-modern-town-planning/) (Cambridge, MA: MIT Press, 1971).

Boyer, Christine, “The City as a Machine,” in [*Dreaming the Rational City: The Myth of American City Planning*](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262021869/dreaming-the-rational-city/) (Cambridge, MA: MIT Press, 1983).

Cacciari, Massimo, [“The Myth of the Growing City,”](https://academic.oup.com/fordham-scholarship-online/book/23610) in *Europe and Empire: On the Political Forms of Globalization* (New York: Fordham University Press, 2016).

Castoriadis, Cornelius, [“The Greek Polis and the Creation of Democracy,”](https://www.pdcnet.org/gfpj/content/gfpj_1983_0009_0002_0079_0115) in *Graduate Faculty Philosophy Journal* 9, no. 2 (Fall 1983): 94–114.

De Bruijne, G.A., “Colonial Cities and the Post-Colonial World,” in Robert Ross and Gerard J. Telkamp, eds., [*Colonial Cities: Essays on Urbanism in a Colonial Context*](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-94-009-6119-7) (Dordrecht: M. Nijhoff, 1985).

de Landa, Manuel, ”Geological History 1000–1700,” in *A Thousand Years of Nonlinear History* (New York: Swerve Editions, 2000), 26–56.

Doxiadis, Constantinos, “Anthropocosmos Model,” *Ekistics* 72 (2005): 430–435.

Easterling, Keller, “Zone,” in *Extrastatecraft: The Power of Infrastructure Space* (London: Verso, 2014), 22–62.

Gandy, Matthew, [“Rethinking Urban Metabolism: Water, Space, and the Modern City,”](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/1360481042000313509) *City* 8, no. 3 (2004): 363–379.

Graeber, David, and David Wengrow, “Imaginary Cities. Eurasia’s First Urbanites—in Mesopotamia, the Indus Valley, Ukraine, and China—and How They Built Cities Without Kings,” in *The Dawn of Everything. A New History of Humanity* (New York: Farrar, Straus and Giroux, 2021), 276–328.

Kipfer, Stephan, [“Mixing It Up: Demolition and Counter-Revolution in Greater Paris,”](https://brill.com/display/book/9789004524910/BP000006.xml) in *Urban Revolutions: Urbanisation and (Neo-)Colonialism in Transatlantic Context* (Leiden: Brill, 2022), 200–239.

Kitto, H.D.F., [“The Polis,”](https://www.taylorfrancis.com/chapters/edit/10.4324/9781315748504-14/polis-kitto) in Jonathan S. Davies and David L. Imbroscio, eds., *Urban Politics* (Los Angeles: SAGE, 2010), 1–12.

Mattern, Shannon, “Urban Technologies,” in *Code and Clay, Data and Dirt: Five Thousand Years of Urban Media*. (Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press, 2017)

Mbembe, Achille, and Sarah Nuttall, [“Writing the World from an African Metropolis,"](https://read.dukeupress.edu/public-culture/article/16/3/347/31776/Writing-the-World-from-an-African-Metropolis) *Public Culture* vol. 16, no. 3 (2004): 347–372.

More, Thomas, [*Utopia*](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2130), excerpts.

Mumford, Lewis, “The Crystallization of the City,” in *The City in History: Its Origins, Transformations, and its Prospects* (New York: Harcourt, Brace, and World Inc, 1961), 29–54.

Norberg-Schulz, Christian, *Meaning in Western Architecture* (New York: Praeger, 1975), 81–88.

Ogilvie, Sheilagh, “Guilds, Cities, and Trade,” in *Institutions and European Trade: Merchant Guilds, 1000–1800* (Cambridge, UK: Cambridge University Press, 2011).

Ong,Aihwa, “Worldling Cities, or the Art of Being Global,” in *Worldling Cities. Asian Experiments in the Art of Being Global*, Ananya Roy and Aihwa Hong, ed. (Chichester, UK: Blackwell Publishing, 2011), 1–26.

Picon, Antoine, [“Nineteenth-Century Urban Cartography and the Scientific Ideal: The Case of Paris,”](https://www.jstor.org/stable/3655289?seq=1) *Osiris* (2003): 135–149.

Rossi, Aldo, “The Roman Forum” and “Monuments: Summary of the Critique of Context,” in *The Architecture of the City* (Cambridge, MA: MIT Press, 1991 [1966])

Rowe, Colin, “Collision City and the Politics of Bricolage,” in *Collage City* (Cambridge, MA: MIT Press, 1978).

Sassen, Saskia, “Medieval City-States and Economic Networks,” in *Territory, Authority, Rights: From Medieval to Global Assemblages* (Princeton, NJ: Princeton University Press, 2008)

Secchi, Bernardo, [“A New Urban Question 3: When, Why and How Some Fundamental Metaphors Were Used,”](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/transcript.9783839423721.123/html?srsltid=AfmBOopgTeZsI5nZmdKDcWUVu_IqQj86GhLGx7BkHmhbTmrgsTbw5_Wu) in *Metaphors in Architecture and Urbanism* vol. 19 (2014): 123–32,

Sennet, Richard, “The Spaces of Democracy,” in *Democracy and Urban Form* (Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 2024)

Sevilla Buitrago, Álvaro, [“Gramsci and Foucault in Central Park: Environmental Hegemonies, Pedagogical Spaces and Integral State Formations,”](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/0263775816658293) *Environment and Planning. D, Society & Space* 35, no.1 (2017): 165–183.

Sevilla-Buitrago, Álvaro, “Planning as a Historical Project,” in *Against The Commons: A Radical History Of Urban Planning* (Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press, 2022), 1–30.

Sheppard, Eric, Helga Leitner, and Anant Maringanti. [“Provincializing Global Urbanism: A Manifesto,”](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/02723638.2013.807977) *Urban Geography* 34.7 (2013): 893–900.

Varzi, Achille C., [“What Is a City?,”](https://philpapers.org/rec/VARWIA-6) *Topoi* 40 (2021): 399–408.

Vitruvius, [*Ten Books on Architecture*](https://archive.org/details/vitruviustenbook00vitruoft) (Cambridge: Harvard University Press, 1914), 17–35.

Wood, Ellen Meiksins, “The Polis and the Peasant Citizen,” in *Peasant-Citizen and Slave. The Foundations of Athenian Democracy* (London: Verso, 1988), 101–125.
