## Assignments

There are four assignments:

- A [précis](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/17-486-japan-and-east-asian-security-fall-2016/pages/assignments/book-precis/) (2 pages)
- A short [scenario paper](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/17-486-japan-and-east-asian-security-fall-2016/pages/assignments/scenario-paper/) (10 pages)
- A longer [briefing paper](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/17-486-japan-and-east-asian-security-fall-2016/pages/assignments/briefing-paper-and-oral-presentation/) (up to 25 pages)
- An [oral presentation](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/17-486-japan-and-east-asian-security-fall-2016/pages/assignments/briefing-paper-and-oral-presentation/) in class
