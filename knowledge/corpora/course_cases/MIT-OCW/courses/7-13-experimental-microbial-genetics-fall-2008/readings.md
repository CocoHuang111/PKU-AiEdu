## Readings

### Relevant Literature

The following journal articles provide background for the research you will be doing in lab.

Banin, E., M. L. Vasil, and E. P. Greenberg. “[Iron and *Pseudomonas Aeruginosa* Biofilm Formation](http://www.pnas.org/content/102/31/11076.abstract).” *PNAS* 102, no. 31 (2005): 11076-81.
