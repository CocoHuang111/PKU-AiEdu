## Assignments "Your vowels" exercise

Due session 5

Using the handout on [Wells’ standard lexical sets (PDF)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/24-914-language-variation-and-change-spring-2019/resources/mit24_914s19_lexicalsets/) as a guide, identify the vowel phonemes in your dialect of English and transcribe their basic realizations. The second examples on the handout are often a better guide to basic realizations than the keywords.
