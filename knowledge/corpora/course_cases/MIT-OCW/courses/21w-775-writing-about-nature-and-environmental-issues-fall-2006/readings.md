## Readings

### Required Texts

Worster, Donald. *Nature’s Economy: A History of Ecological Ideas*. 2nd ed. Cambridge, UK: Cambridge University Press, 1994. ISBN: 9780521468343.

Duane, Daniel. *Caught Inside: A Surfer’s Year on the California Coast*. New York, NY: North Point Press, 1997. ISBN: 9780865475090.

Wilson, Edward, and Stephen Kellert, eds. *The Biophilia Hypothesis*. Washington, DC: Island Press, 1995. ISBN: 9781559631471.

Kolbert, Elizabeth. *Field Notes from a Catastrophe: Man, Nature, and Climate Change*. New York, NY: Bloomsbury U.S.A., 2006. ISBN: 9781596911307.

Sullivan, Robert. *Rats: Observations on the History and Habitat of the City’s Most Unwanted Inhabitants*. Reprint ed. New York, NY: Bloomsbury U.S.A., 2005. ISBN: 9781582344775.
