## Projects

A final project is required of each student for them to get a good grade. The project can be studying a single paper in depth, implementing/extending an algorithm, or proving theoretical results on a problem of the student’s choice. Below are some sample student projects.

Burrows-Wheeler Transforms in Linear Time and Linear Bits ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-417-introduction-to-computational-molecular-biology-fall-2004/resources/cox_talk/)) (Courtesy of Russ Cox. Used with permission.)

Sampling Good Motifs with Markov Chains ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-417-introduction-to-computational-molecular-biology-fall-2004/resources/cjp_project/)) (Courtesy of Christopher J. Peikert. Used with permission.)

Robust Clustering Techniques in Bioinformatic ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-417-introduction-to-computational-molecular-biology-fall-2004/resources/beverly_cluster/)) (Courtesy of Robert Edward Beverly. Used with permission.)
