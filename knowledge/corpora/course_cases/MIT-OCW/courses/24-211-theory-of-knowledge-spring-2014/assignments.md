### Papers

#### Midterm

Your midterm paper should address the following question:

*Do we have justification to form beliefs on the basis of perception only if we have independent justification to believe that perception is reliable?*

[Midterm paper guidelines and instructions (PDF)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/24-211-theory-of-knowledge-spring-2014/resources/mit24_211s11_midterm/)

Student example: [“Wright, skepticism and entitlement” (PDF)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/24-211-theory-of-knowledge-spring-2014/resources/mit24_211s11_wright/)

#### Final

Your final paper should address the following question:

*Does the lottery paradox show that we don’t know much about the external world?*

[Final paper guidelines and instructions(PDF)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/24-211-theory-of-knowledge-spring-2014/resources/mit24_211s11_finalpaper/)

Student example: [“The Contexualist Solution to the Lottery Paradox” (PDF)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/24-211-theory-of-knowledge-spring-2014/resources/mit24_211s11_contextualsol/)
