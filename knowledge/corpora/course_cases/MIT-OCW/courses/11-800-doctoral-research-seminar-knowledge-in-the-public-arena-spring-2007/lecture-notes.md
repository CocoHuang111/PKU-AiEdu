## Lecture Notes

| SES # | TOPICS |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Introduction <br> Overview of the course — the faculty, objectives, format, and requirements. First lectures: Knowledge use in theory and practice, perspectives from inside and outside academia. ( <br> [PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/11-800-doctoral-research-seminar-knowledge-in-the-public-arena-spring-2007/resources/lec1/) <br> ) |
| 2 | Models of knowledge production and decision-making <br> The rational policy/planning model, muddling through, deliberation and social learning. |
| 3 | Agendas and the policy process <br> The importance of agenda-setting and other “pre-decision” processes in policymaking. The Kingdon model and its critics; the role of narrative. |
| 4 | Frames and persuasion <br> Storytelling, metaphor, and frames. Alignment among frames, institutional context, and policy and political streams. ( <br> [PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/11-800-doctoral-research-seminar-knowledge-in-the-public-arena-spring-2007/resources/lec4/) <br> ) <br> Guest: Jal Mehta, Harvard Graduate School of Education |
| 5 | Paradigms and fads <br> Design and urban form, urban utopias, popular culture and the Good City. ( <br> [PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/11-800-doctoral-research-seminar-knowledge-in-the-public-arena-spring-2007/resources/lec5/) <br> ) <br> Guest: Lawrence Vale, MIT |
| 6 | Diffusion of innovation <br> Creating and diffusing innovation, “structured” diffusion, replication and mimicking. ( <br> [PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/11-800-doctoral-research-seminar-knowledge-in-the-public-arena-spring-2007/resources/lec6/) <br> ) |
| 7 | Case of anti-poverty policy and research <br> The Moving to Opportunity experiment as a social policy case, political and fiscal context, images of ghetto poverty, the Hurricane Katrina media effect. ( <br> [PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/11-800-doctoral-research-seminar-knowledge-in-the-public-arena-spring-2007/resources/lec7/) <br> ) <br> Guest: Jeffrey Liebman, Harvard Kennedy School of Government |
| 8 | Science in environmental policy disputes <br> Science-intensive disputes, “experts for hire”, joint fact finding. <br> Guest: Lawrence Susskind, MIT and the Consensus Building Institute |
| 9 | Action learning and practice <br> Action learning, embedded or “social” learning, theories of practice, communities of practice (knowledge networks). ( <br> [PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/11-800-doctoral-research-seminar-knowledge-in-the-public-arena-spring-2007/resources/lec9/) <br> ) |
| 10 | The politics and use of evaluation research <br> Truth tests and utility tests, demonstration theory of social change, media and public consumption. ( <br> [PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/11-800-doctoral-research-seminar-knowledge-in-the-public-arena-spring-2007/resources/lec10/) <br> ) |
| 11 | Research writing for non-academic readers <br> Research briefs, communication channels, writing support, editing styles. ( <br> [PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/11-800-doctoral-research-seminar-knowledge-in-the-public-arena-spring-2007/resources/lec11/) <br> ) |
