## Syllabus

### Course Meeting Times

Lectures: 2 sessions / week, 1.5 hours / session

Recitations: 1 session / week, 1 hour / session

### Required Text Book

Huang, Kerson. *Introduction to Statistical Physics*. London, UK: Taylor & Francis, 2001. ISBN: 9780748409419.

### Prerequisites

Statistical Physics I ([8.044](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-044-statistical-physics-i-spring-2013)), Quantum Physics II ([8.05](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-05-quantum-physics-ii-fall-2013))

### Grade

| activities | percentages |
| --- | --- |
| Homework (once a week) | 40% |
| Quiz 1 (open book) | 15% |
| Quiz 2 (open book) | 15% |
| Final Exam (open book) | 30% |

### Homework

Homework is handed out every week and is due one week later in class.
