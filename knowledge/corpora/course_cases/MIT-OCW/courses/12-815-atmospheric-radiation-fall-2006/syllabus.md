## Syllabus

### Course Meeting Times

Lectures: 1 session / week, 2 hours / session

Labs: 1 session / week, 2 hours / session

### Prerequisites

Physical Chemistry ([5.61](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/5-61-physical-chemistry-fall-2007)), Advanced Calculus for Engineers ([18.075](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-075-advanced-calculus-for-engineers-fall-2004)), or permission of instructor.

### Grading

Based on grades from 6 exercises (weighted by degree of difficulty).

### Required Textbook

Liou, Kuo-Nan. *An Introduction to Atmospheric Radiation*. 2nd ed. Burlington, MA: Academic Press, 2002. ISBN: 9780124514515.

### Reference Textbook

Goody, R., and Y. Yung. *Atmospheric Radiation*. New York, NY: Oxford University Press, 1995. ISBN: 9780195102918.
