## Course Description

This course explores formal and informal modes of writing nonfiction prose, with extensive practice in composition, revision, and editing. There will be readings in the literature of the essay from the Renaissance to the present, with an emphasis on modern writers. Classes alternate between discussion of published readings and workshops on student work.
