## Syllabus

### Course Meeting Times

Lectures: 3 sessions / week, 1 hour / session

### Prerequisites:

Differential Equations ([18.03](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-03-differential-equations-spring-2006)) or Honors Differential Equations ([18.034](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-034-honors-differential-equations-spring-2004)). Knowledge of programming in Fortran, C, or Matlab® is helpful.

### Textbook:

Burden, Richard L., and J. Douglas Faires. *Numerical Analysis*. 7th ed. Belmont, CA: Brooks Cole, 2000. ISBN: 0534382169.

### Problem sets:

Twelve, due Fridays at lecture.

### Grading:

100% based on problem sets.
