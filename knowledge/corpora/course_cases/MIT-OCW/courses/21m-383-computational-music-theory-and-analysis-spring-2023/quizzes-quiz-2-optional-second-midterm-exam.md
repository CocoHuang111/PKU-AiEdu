## Quiz 2: Optional Second Midterm Exam

*OPTIONAL*

The format of Quiz 2 is two long-answer/short-essay questions (like Quiz 1). One of the questions will be the same as Quiz 1 (Global Rule, Equivalence, 3 Contexts), and one will be taken from either Krumhansl’s Probe-Tone Experiments, David Huron’s *Sweet Anticiation*, [the reading on the ILLIAC Suite](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21m-383-computational-music-theory-and-analysis-spring-2023/pages/class-22-history-of-the-illiac-suite/), [George Lewis’s lecture on Improvisation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wDP8FsjyCaA), or on a short topics including Leon Hackleroad’s *How not to Mix Mathematics and Music*, and [my “Tipping the Iceberg” video](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21m-383-computational-music-theory-and-analysis-spring-2023/pages/class-32-asynchronous-material-on-computational-music-theory-and-music-history/) about the number of lost songs from the Middle Ages.
