## Assignments

| SES # | TOPICS | ASSIGNMENTS |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Introductions <br> Proposal Specific Writing Issues <br> Introduction to Information Sources | Do this Before Ses #2 <br> Find a thesis advisor; read his/her published work; let your thesis advisor know the timeline for your proposal work. <br> Fill out a thesis intent form and turn it in to the mechanical engineering undergraduate office. (If you have not done so already.) <br> Get a thesis notebook. <br> Scope the 2.ThA archives for similar projects; surf the web for similar masters/PhD proposals; use these sources to determine what sorts of background information you will need for your thesis; jot down these information requirements in a format that is legible to others. (In your notebook would be fine.) <br> Fill out the “MadLib” thesis proposal outline. <br> Complete the research assignment; see handout. |
| 2 | Progress Reports <br> Follow up on Information Sources <br> Information Sources “Hands-on” Exercise <br> Graphics | Do this Before Ses #3 <br> Prepare a significant draft of your thesis proposal for scrutiny in class. Emphasize the introduction and background sections. <br> Prepare the graphics that will become your presentation slides. |
| 3 | Proposal Workshop <br> Graphics Show and Tell <br> About Oral Presentations | Do this Two Full Days Before Ses #4 <br> Based on the feedback in class, revise your proposal draft; turn the draft in via e-mail to the instructor. <br> Do this Before Ses #4 <br> Prepare to deliver a 5 minute overview of your thesis project. Emphasize problem, motivation, context, and structure. Let the audience know what it is that they are supposed to get out of the talk. |
| 4 | Shorter, Less Formal Presentations <br> Review of Presentations <br> Possible Additional Topic(s) | Do this Before Ses #5 <br> Further revise your proposal based on the instructor’s feedback for workshop in class and formal peer review. <br> Prepare the slides for your longer, more formal presentation. |
| 5 | Workshop II | Do this One Day Before Ses #6 <br> Peer review two of your classmate’s proposals. <br> Do this Before Ses #6 and #7 <br> Prepare to deliver your longer, more formal presentation. <br> Do this Before Ses #7 <br> Further revise your proposal; this draft should be nearly perfect. |
| 6-7 | Longer, More Formal Presentations <br> Subject Evaluation Forms | Do this Before the End of Registration Day <br> Turn in your signed thesis proposal cover sheet and your thesis proposal (hardcopy and e-copy) to the instructor. |
