## Assignments

### Response Papers

Participants will write five short response papers (750 - 1000 words) that critically discuss a given week’s readings. To ensure unbiased grading, please put your name on a separate page at the end of the paper.

### Exemplary Response Papers

The following five response papers are by Kristin Fabbe and are used with permission.

Paper 1: “Repairing” Classical Theories of Secularization: Two Approaches ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/17-522-politics-and-religion-fall-2006/resources/paper1/))

Paper 2: Continuity and the Emergence of Nationalism: Recognizing the Political Dimension of Church-State Relations ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/17-522-politics-and-religion-fall-2006/resources/paper2/))

Paper 3: Religion and Political Legitimacy: Religions as Unitary Actors? ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/17-522-politics-and-religion-fall-2006/resources/paper3/))

Paper 4: When Cultural Prejudices Parade as Social Science: The Problem of Selection Bias ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/17-522-politics-and-religion-fall-2006/resources/paper4/))

Paper 5: The Problem of the PKK: Supplementing Pape’s Theory with Thick Description ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/17-522-politics-and-religion-fall-2006/resources/paper5/))

### Leading Discussion

Each seminar participant will serve as discussion leader for a class session in a week for which they have not written a response paper.
