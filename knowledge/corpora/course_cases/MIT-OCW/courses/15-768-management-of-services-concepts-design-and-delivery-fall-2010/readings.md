## Readings

Discussion questions for all assigned readings and cases can be found in the [Assignments](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-768-management-of-services-concepts-design-and-delivery-fall-2010/pages/assignments/) section of this course. Students must read all the assigned case studies, but are required to complete write-ups for only three of their choosing. A list of optional reading materials can be found in the [Syllabus](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-768-management-of-services-concepts-design-and-delivery-fall-2010/pages/syllabus/).
