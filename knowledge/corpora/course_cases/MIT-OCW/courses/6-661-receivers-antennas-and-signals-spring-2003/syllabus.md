## Syllabus

### Homework

Assignments are due weekly. They represent approximately 20% of the grade.

### Quizzes

Quiz 1 is after Lecture 14 and Quiz 2 is after Lecture 24. Each quiz is 1.5 hours in duration. There is no final exam.

### Course Texts

Draft of [textbook](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-661-receivers-antennas-and-signals-spring-2003/pages/readings/), plus [slides](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-661-receivers-antennas-and-signals-spring-2003/pages/lecture-notes/) handed out prior to lecture.
