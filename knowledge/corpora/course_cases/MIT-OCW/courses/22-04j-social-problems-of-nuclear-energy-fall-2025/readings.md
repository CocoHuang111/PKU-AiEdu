## Readings

### Introduction

#### Session 1: A Quick History of Nuclear Energy

- Weinberg, Alvin M. [“Nuclear Power and Public Perception.”](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-94-009-2207-5_10) Chapter 9 in *Risk, Organizations, and Society*. Kluwer Academic Publishers, 1991. ISBN: 9780792391180.
- Manjoo, Farhad. [“Nuclear Power Still Doesn’t Make Much Sense.”](https://www.nytimes.com/2022/09/16/opinion/nuclear-power-still-doesnt-make-much-sense.html) *The New York Times*, September 16, 2022.
- Jasper, James M. “Elite Discretion in Three Countries.” Chapter 10 in [*Nuclear Politics: Energy and the State in the United States, Sweden, and France*](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctt7zv8c6?turn_away=true)*.* Princeton University Press, 1990. ISBN: 9780691609201.

#### Session 2: How Do Reactors Work?

Optional reading: For students with little-to-no prior exposure to nuclear energy, start here:

- Shultis, J. Kenneth, and Richard E. Faw. “Nuclear Power.” Chapter 11 in [*Fundamentals of Nuclear Science and Engineering.* (PDF)](https://dl.icdst.org/pdfs/files4/03ee780f3512628eb0bfd60d3a46fa4e.pdf) Marcel Dekker, Inc., 2002. ISBN: 9780824708344.
- International Atomic Energy Agency. [“Fusion Energy.” (PDF)](http://large.stanford.edu/courses/2024/ph241/moise1/docs/iaea-bull-may21.pdf) *IAEA Bulletin*, May 2021.

Optional reading: For students not familiar with the commercial reactor designs (e.g., Gen II, III, IV):

- Goldberg, Stephen M., and Robert Rosner. [*Nuclear Reactors: Generation to Generation* (PDF)](https://www.amacad.org/sites/default/files/academy/pdfs/nuclearReactors.pdf). American Academies of Arts and Sciences, 2011.

#### Session 3: How We Think About Climate Change

- Pindyck, Robert S. [“Climate Change Policy: What Do the Models Tell Us?”](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/jel.51.3.860) *Journal of Economic Literature* 51, no. 3 (2013): 860–872.

### Part II: Nuclear Safety

#### Session 13: Introduction to Externalities and Regulation

- Wing, Steve. “[Objectivity and Ethics in Environmental Science](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1241729/).” *Environmental Health Perspectives* 111, no. 14 (November 2003): 1809–1818.
- Wellock, Thomas R. “[Engineering Uncertainty and Bureaucratic Crisis and the Atomic Energy Commission, 1964–1973](https://www.jstor.org/stable/41682744).” *Technology and Culture* 53, no. 4 (October 2012): 846–884.
- “[Appendix L: Factoring the Costs of Severe Nuclear Accidents into Backfit Decisions](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK253929/)” in *Lessons Learned from the Fukushima Nuclear Accident for Improving Safety of U.S. Nuclear Plants*. National Academies of Sciences, 2014.

#### Session 14: Radiation Biology

- Caufield, Catherine. “Confusion and Acrimony.” Chapter 16 in [*Multiple Exposures: Chronicles of the Radiation Age*](https://www.amazon.com/Multiple-Exposures-Chronicles-Radiation-Age/dp/0226097854) University of Chicago Press, 1990. ISBN: 9780226097855.
- Fajardo, Luis Felipe, Morgan Berthrong, and Robert Anderson. “Basic radiation, physics, chemistry, and biology” Chapter 1 in [*Radiation Pathology*](https://www.amazon.com/Radiation-Pathology-Luis-Felipe-Fajardo/dp/0195110234)*.* Oxford University Press, 2001. ISBN: 9780195110234. (**Note:** When reading the above, you should know the term “neoplasm” means a cancer of either a solid (i.e., a tumor) or disaggregated (i.e., leukemia) form.)
- International Committee on Radiation Protection (ICRP). “[Publication 99: Low-Dose Extrapolation of Radiation-Related Cancer Risk](https://www.icrp.org/publication.asp?id=ICRP%20Publication%2099).” *Annals of the ICRP* 35 no. 4, (2005): 5–39.

#### Session 15: LNT and Model Selection

- The National Diet of Japan. [“The Official Report of The Fukushima Nuclear Accident Independent Investigation Commission Risk Analysis.” (PDF)](https://warp.ndl.go.jp/web/20121025094421/naiic.go.jp/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/NAIIC_report_hi_res10.pdf) (2012). Pages 10–21 and 26–44.

#### Session 16: Safety Summary

I’m Not Surprised](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/opinions/2002/04/28/heard-about-the-near-accident-at-the-ohio-nuclear-plant-im-not-surprised/9d015883-8bc8-44a8-bc2b-d1ff0aea0915/).” *Washington Post*, April 28<sup>th</sup>, 2002.
