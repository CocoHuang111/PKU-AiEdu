## Lecture Notes

These notes are somewhat rough: they may contain some mistakes, so please use caution when reading them.

Some notes about Grassmannians. ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-727-topics-in-algebraic-geometry-intersection-theory-on-moduli-spaces-spring-2006/resources/week1/))

Some notes about the construction of the Hilbert scheme. Included are some notes on G.I.T. and the construction of the moduli spaces. ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-727-topics-in-algebraic-geometry-intersection-theory-on-moduli-spaces-spring-2006/resources/const/))

Some notes containing a brief survey of the cohomology of the moduli space of curves and the Harer-Zagier formula for the orbifold Euler characteristic. I added a few words outlining other work of Harer on the stability of the cohomology of the moduli space of curves. ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-727-topics-in-algebraic-geometry-intersection-theory-on-moduli-spaces-spring-2006/resources/homology/))

Some notes describing the Picard group of the moduli space of curves. ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-727-topics-in-algebraic-geometry-intersection-theory-on-moduli-spaces-spring-2006/resources/picard/))

Some notes about the Kodaira dimension of the moduli space of curves. ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-727-topics-in-algebraic-geometry-intersection-theory-on-moduli-spaces-spring-2006/resources/generaltype/)) You should also read Chapter 5 and 6F of Harris, J., and I. Morrison. *Moduli of Curves*. New York, NY: Springer-Verlag, 1998, p. 366. ISBN: 0387984380. (U.S.)

Some notes on the Kontsevich moduli space of genus zero stable maps. ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-727-topics-in-algebraic-geometry-intersection-theory-on-moduli-spaces-spring-2006/resources/kontsevich/))
