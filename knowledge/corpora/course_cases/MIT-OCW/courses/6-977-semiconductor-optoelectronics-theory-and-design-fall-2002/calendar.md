## Calendar

| Class # | Topics |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Background <br> carrier distributions, pn junctions, carrier injection |
| 2 | Photodetectors <br> absorption in bulk semiconductors, displacement currents, gain-bandwidth limits |
| 3 | Modulators <br> absorption in quantum wells, quantum confined Stark effect |
| 4 | Optical amplifiers <br> population inversion and gain, non-radiative recombination |
| 5 | Optical amplifiers <br> coupled electron-photon rate equations, gain saturation, optical confinement |
| 6 | Lasers <br> Fabry-Perot resonators, lasing threshold |
| 7 | Heterostructure materials <br> optical and electrical properties of alloys, heterostructure band alignment |
| 8 | Distributed feedback (DFB) and surface emitting laser resonators <br> T-matrix formalism and coupled mode theory, grating based resonators |
| 9 | Review, <br> midterm |
| 10 | Tunable optics <br> chirp in semiconductor media, electro-optic effects |
| 11 | Modulation <br> small-signal and large signal analysis |
| 12 | Noise in optoelectronic devices <br> Langevin theory, shot noise limits, relative intensity noise (RIN) of lasers |
| 13 | Systems <br> WDM system design, noise and power budget for fiber optic systems |
