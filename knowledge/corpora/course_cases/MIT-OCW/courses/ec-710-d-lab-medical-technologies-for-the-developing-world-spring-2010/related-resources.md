## Related Resources

Course instructor Jose Gomez-Marquez and colleague Anna Young co-founded and continue to run the Little Devices Lab, which operated out of MIT for many years. Supporting work and projects similar to those highlighted in this course, the Lab empowers healthcare workers to invent and build their own customized, cost-effective medical devices for a range of applications from diagnostics to microfluidics to drug delivery.

Please see [Little Devices Lab @ Emory](https://littledeviceslab.org/) to learn about the Lab’s current and ongoing work.

Please see [RES.HST-001 MIT Little Devices Lab (archived)](https://web.archive.org/web/20251115054237/http:/jfgm.scripts.mit.edu/littledeviceslab/) for more about the Lab’s work during its years at MIT.
