## Calendar

Many of the class sessions are each built around a business case study, with the decisions and situations encountered by the featured company as a driver of in-class discussion. Guiding questions for analyzing each of the cases as well as supporting material are provided in the [Readings](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-229-managing-global-integration-spring-2012/pages/readings/) section.
