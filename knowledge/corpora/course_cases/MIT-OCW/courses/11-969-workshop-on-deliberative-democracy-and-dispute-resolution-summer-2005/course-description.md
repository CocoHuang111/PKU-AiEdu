## Course Description

The Workshop on Deliberative Democracy and Dispute Resolution, sponsored by the Program on Negotiation at Harvard Law School and [*The Flora and William Hewlett Foundation*](http://www.hewlett.org/), is a two-day conference that brings together dispute resolution professionals and political theorists in the field of deliberative democracy.
