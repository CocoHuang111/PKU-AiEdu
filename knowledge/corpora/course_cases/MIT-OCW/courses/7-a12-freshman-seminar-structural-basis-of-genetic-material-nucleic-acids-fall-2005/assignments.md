## Assignments

Each student is required to write a two-page summary on one paper of his/her choice from the reading assignments given in the [readings](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/7-a12-freshman-seminar-structural-basis-of-genetic-material-nucleic-acids-fall-2005/pages/readings/) section. The students will present their summaries to the class. Use of slides during the presentation is optional.
