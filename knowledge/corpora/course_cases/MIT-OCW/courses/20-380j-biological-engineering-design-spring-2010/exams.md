## Exams

This class has one exam at mid-term, on the six technical papers assigned for group presentations. See the [readings](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/20-380j-biological-engineering-design-spring-2010/pages/other/) for Sessions 7-8.

Details:

- The exam is closed book, no notes.
- You will have 2 hours to complete it.
- You will be provided with the abstracts of the six papers you have read.
- You will not need a calculator.
