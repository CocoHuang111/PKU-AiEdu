## Readings

Asterisked references are more important to the course. The following is a tentative list of topics that will be covered in this course.

### Required Text

[Hamilton] = Hamilton, James D. *Time Series Analysis*. Princeton University Press, 1994. ISBN: 9780691042893.

### Recommended Texts

[Brockwell and Davis] = Brockwell, Peter, and Richard Davis. *Time Series: Theory and Methods*. Springer-Verlag, 1991. ISBN: 9780387974293. [Preview with [Google Books](http://books.google.com/books?id=ZW_ThhYQiXIC&pg=PAfrontcover)]
