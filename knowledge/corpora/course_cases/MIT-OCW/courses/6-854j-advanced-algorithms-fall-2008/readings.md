## Readings

There is no textbook required for the course. [Lecture notes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-854j-advanced-algorithms-fall-2008/pages/lecture-notes/) are available for the current term as well as selected lecture notes from a [previous term](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-854j-advanced-algorithms-fall-2008/pages/study-materials/). Reference textbooks for each topic are listed in the table below. Lecture notes also contain references.
