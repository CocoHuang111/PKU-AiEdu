## Readings

The required readings are indicated with a star symbol (*). The remaining readings include useful references, which might be covered briefly during the course.

### Lecture 1: Introduction

- Luttrell, D., T. Atkinson, and H. Rosenblum, “[Assessing the Costs and Consequences of the 2007–09 Financial Crisis and Its Aftermath](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/title/economic-letter-6362/assessing-costs-consequences-2007-09-financial-crisis-aftermath-607683),” *Dallas Fed Economic Letter* 8(7), September 2013.
- Allen, F., E. Carletti, J.P. Krahnen, and M. Tyrell, “An Introduction to Liquidity and Crises,” in [*Liquidity and Crises*](https://www.google.com/books/edition/Liquidity_and_Crises/T_-n33Umb5UC?hl=en&gbpv=1&dq=liquidity+and+crises&printsec=frontcover), (Eds.) (same authors), Oxford University Press, 3–32, 2011.
- Ball, L.M., “[The Fed and Lehman Brothers](https://www.google.com/books/edition/The_Fed_and_Lehman_Brothers/fvpTDwAAQBAJ?hl=en&gbpv=1&dq=the+fed+and+lehman+brothers&printsec=frontcover),” Cambridge University Press, June 2018.
- Caballero, R.J., “[Macroeconomics After the Crisis: Time to Deal with the Pretense-of-Knowledge Syndrome](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/jep.24.4.85).” *Journal of Economic Perspectives*, 85–102, Fall 2010.
- Blanchard, O.J., G. Dell’Ariccia, and P. Mauro, “[Rethinking Macroeconomic Policy](https://www.imf.org/external/pubs/ft/spn/2010/spn1003.pdf),” *Journal of Money, Credit and Banking* 42(s1), 199–215, September 2010.
- Caballero, R.J., “[Crisis and Reform: Managing Systemic Risk” (PDF)](https://economics.mit.edu/sites/default/files/publications/XI-COSTA-LECTURE-CABALLERO-PRINTED.pdf), *Rivista di Politica Economica* 99(4), 9–64, October-December 2009.
- Calomiris, Ch.W., and M.S. Jaremiski, “[The Puzzling Persistence of Financial Crises](https://www.nber.org/papers/w32213),” *NBER Working Paper* 32212, March 2024.
