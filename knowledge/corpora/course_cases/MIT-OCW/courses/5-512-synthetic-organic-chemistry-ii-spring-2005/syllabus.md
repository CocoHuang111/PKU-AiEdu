## Syllabus

### Course Meeting Times

Lectures: 3 sessions / week, 1 hour / session

### Course Texts

Gawley, Robert E., and Jeffrey Aubé. *Principles of Asymmetric Synthesis.* Oxford, UK: Pergamon, 1996. ISBN: 9780080418759.

Lecture Notes: *Methods for the Asymmetric Synthesis of Complex Organic* Molecules, Daniel J. O’Leary, Associate Professor of Chemistry, Pomona College (2001).

### Grading

Course grades will be based on two exams scheduled during the semester. There is no final exam.
