## Assignments Journal Reflection #3

Write about a classroom observation you have done this semester in terms of the 24 teaching techniques discussed in [*The* *Skillful Teacher*](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/11-129-educational-theory-and-practice-i-fall-2011/pages/readings/) (how to make a lesson more efficient and effective). You are unlikely to have seen 24 techniques in one class. Just focus on what you DID see.

- Describe in detail what happened in the classroom (narrative description of lesson).
- What skills did the teacher employ to make it a good lesson? What went well?
- Could the lesson have been improved (what would you have been done differently and why)?

Be sure to address all three points in your paper.
