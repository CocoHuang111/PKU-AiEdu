## Lecture Notes

### Notes and Worksheets

The session 1 notes ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-189-a-gentle-introduction-to-programming-using-python-january-iap-2008/resources/notes1/)) include the syllabus, some administrivia and an introductory tutorial to Python.

Exam 1 (Session 5) covered everything through class 3: variables, types, operators, functions, conditionals and loops. The solutions double as notes for sessions 1 through 3. ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-189-a-gentle-introduction-to-programming-using-python-january-iap-2008/resources/exam1_soln/))

The session 6 homework ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-189-a-gentle-introduction-to-programming-using-python-january-iap-2008/resources/notes6/)) doubles as notes for classes 4 through 6.

The optional session 7 homework ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-189-a-gentle-introduction-to-programming-using-python-january-iap-2008/resources/notes7/)) serves as useful practice with lists.

The session 8 notes ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-189-a-gentle-introduction-to-programming-using-python-january-iap-2008/resources/notes8/)) cover dictionaries and their usage.

The session 9 worksheet ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-189-a-gentle-introduction-to-programming-using-python-january-iap-2008/resources/notes9/)) reviews common errors and good programming practices.

### Handouts

Handout 1 ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-189-a-gentle-introduction-to-programming-using-python-january-iap-2008/resources/handout1/)) reviewed while loops and the basics of for loops.

Handout 2 ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-189-a-gentle-introduction-to-programming-using-python-january-iap-2008/resources/handout2/)) was a heavy review before the midterm, covering functions, lists, for loops and tuples.

Handout 3 ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-189-a-gentle-introduction-to-programming-using-python-january-iap-2008/resources/handout3/)) reviewed objects.

Handout 4 ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-189-a-gentle-introduction-to-programming-using-python-january-iap-2008/resources/handout4/)) reviewed dictionaries and gave guidance on part 1 of the Web indexer lab.

Handout 5 ([PDF](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-189-a-gentle-introduction-to-programming-using-python-january-iap-2008/resources/handout5/)) was a walkthrough for part 2 of the Web indexer lab, and covered compound dictionaries.
