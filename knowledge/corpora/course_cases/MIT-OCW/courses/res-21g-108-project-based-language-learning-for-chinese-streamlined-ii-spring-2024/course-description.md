## Course Description

This is the student project for the Spring 2024 MIT course [*21G.108 Chinese II (Streamlined)*](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/21g-108-chinese-ii-streamlined-spring-2015/). In the project, Chinese heritage language students explore various topics, including the meaning of their Chinese names. Based on their discoveries, each student creates an online book.
